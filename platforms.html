WHAT A FORCED RETURN COSTS YOU, BY PLATFORM

The same ruined item costs a different amount depending on where you sold it. Here are the 2026 rules, platform by platform, with the loss formula and a worked example for each.

eBay — the three-front loss

  • Final value fee 13.6% (books 15.3%, cards/coins 13.25%); per-order fee $0.30 (≤$10) / $0.40 (>$10).
  • "Not as described / damaged / wrong item": the 13.6% is refunded but the per-order fee is kept; you also pay return shipping.
  • Buyer changed mind: both refunded. eBay stepped in against you: nothing refunded.
Worked: $60 sale, INAD, $8.50 label, item now worth $25 → $43.90 lost.

Poshmark — fees reverse, value doesn't

  • Commission 20% (or $2.95 under $15); reversed on an approved return; prepaid label provided.
  • Fit / change-of-mind returns go through Seel: Seel refunds the buyer, you keep your earnings — $0 loss.
  • Your real loss is the item's value if it returns worn, stained, or swapped.
Worked: $40 dress returned stained, now worth $15 → $25.00 lost.

Mercari — the 10% is back, and heavy items bite

  • 10% selling fee (price + buyer shipping) since Jan 6, 2025; no separate seller processing fee.
  • Returns under 50 lbs: free prepaid label. Over 50 lbs: you pay the return label within 3 days.
  • The 3.6% buyer-protection fee is on the buyer, not you.
Worked: 60 lb item forced return → you buy the label (~$16+) plus the item's lost value.

Depop — fees refund, so loss = value + shipping

  • No selling fee (US/UK/AUS); processing 3.3% + $0.45 (Depop Payments).
  • Refund via Depop Payments and all fees are reversed automatically.
  • US buyers get a prepaid return label; returns ship within 7 days.
Worked: $30 tee returned damaged, now worth $10 → $20.00 lost.

Whatnot — the gross pool trap

  • Commission 8% on item price (4% for Coins & Money and Pallets). Eligible categories get 0% on the portion above $1,500.
  • Payment processing is 2.9% + $0.30 on the GROSS order value — item + buyer shipping + buyer tax. This is the trap: you pay processing on money you never pocket.
  • The $0.30 fixed fee is charged per checkout, so a 5-item bundle from 5 separate bids costs you 5 × $0.30 = $1.50.
  • Carrier weight clawbacks are deducted days after your stream when USPS/UPS re-weighs your package.
Worked: $100 card with $8 buyer shipping + $5 tax + $4.50 weight clawback → $12.73 in fees. Advertised 8%, real take 12.7%.

Vinted — the $0 anchor

  • $0 seller fees — no commission, no processing, no listing fee. You keep 100% of your listed price.
  • The BUYER pays the platform: $0.70 + 5% Buyer Protection fee plus the prepaid shipping label.
  • Returns are handled inside the app; the seller typically pays $0 in return shipping on approved claims.
  • Optional paid boosts (Item Boost, Wardrobe Spotlight) are the only seller cost.
Worked: $50 item sold on Vinted → you keep $50.00. Same item on Poshmark leaves you with $40. Vinted is your anchor.
