About CLAWBACK

I didn't build CLAWBACK because I wanted to start a blog. I built it because I was tired of losing money and not knowing exactly where it went.

The Origin Story

Like a lot of resellers, I started flipping clothes and vintage items on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, and Depop to make some extra income. At first, the math seemed simple: buy for $10, sell for $40, make $30 profit. Right?

Wrong.

Then came the platform fees. Then the payment processing fees. Then the shipping label costs. And the worst part: the forced returns. A buyer would claim a jacket "didn't fit," I'd be forced to pay for the return shipping label, and when the item came back, it had a stain on it. I had lost the original sale, lost the shipping money both ways, and now had a damaged item I couldn't resell at full price.

I realized that the "profit" the apps showed me at the top of the screen was a lie. The real profit was hidden in the margins, the fees, and the returns. I started tracking every single forced return in a spreadsheet to see my true loss. That spreadsheet eventually became the CLAWBACK Calculator.

Our Mission

CLAWBACK exists to give resellers the unvarnished truth about their margins. We break down the complex, 40-page return policies of major marketplaces into plain English. We show you exactly what a return costs you, what fees actually come back, and how to protect your business from scams and "wardrobing" buyers.

Our Promise to You

1. We are independent. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or sponsored by eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, or Depop. We tell you the truth about their policies, even when it's ugly.

2. We are free. The core calculator and our deep-dive guides will always be 100% free to use. No email signup required.

3. We are resellers first. Every guide on this site is written from the perspective of someone who actually packs boxes, prints labels, and deals with buyer disputes.

Thanks for being here. Now go list something, and remember to track your true margins.

— The CLAWBACK Team
