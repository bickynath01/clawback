WHY CLAWBACK EXISTS

Because a seller held a ruined jacket, typed "how much did I actually lose?" into Google, and got back a four-year-old forum thread where strangers did mental math. That seller was us. This tool is the answer we couldn't find.

The problem nobody calculates

Every calculator on the internet answers "what will I make if this sells?" None of them answer the question that actually keeps resellers up at night: "the buyer returned it damaged — what did that cost me?" The loss is real — kept fees, forced return shipping, and an item that can't be resold at the same price — but it's invisible, so sellers absorb it silently, one return at a time.

CLAWBACK exists to put a dollar figure on that loss, tell you whether it's worth fighting, and hand you the appeal letter and the evidence checklist to win.

Our promise

  • Free, no signup, no login. Your numbers are computed in your browser and never leave it.
  • Sourced, not guessed. Every rate comes from official marketplace help pages, re-verified quarterly. See the Sources page.
  • Independent. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, or Depop.
  • Honest about uncertainty. Where a platform's refund behavior varies by reason, the calculator asks you what happened and applies the matching rule — and says so.

Who it's for

The individual reseller — the person with one returned item and one frustrated question, not a store analyst with a spreadsheet of a thousand orders. If you've ever eaten a return loss in silence, this was built for you.
