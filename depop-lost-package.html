Depop Lost Package: What Sellers Should Do

Quick answer: If you used a Depop Shipping label and met the dispatch deadline, Depop Seller Protection may cover the loss (up to $300/£250/A$450) after confirmation. If you shipped independently, you are generally responsible for refunding the buyer and must pursue compensation from the carrier yourself.

LAST UPDATED: 28 OCTOBER 2026

By CLAWBACK Editorial Team · Independent reseller research

You shipped the order. Now the tracking has stopped updating, the buyer says the package never arrived, or the carrier says it was delivered when the buyer insists it wasn't.

You're left wondering what happens next. Will Depop refund the buyer? Will you lose the item and the money? Can Depop cover the loss? And what if you paid for your own shipping instead of using a Depop label?

These are difficult situations for a seller because you can't personally control what happens to a parcel after it leaves your hands. But you still need to know what to do, what evidence to keep and how the shipping method affects your options.

The most important thing is to establish what happened to the package and check which shipping rules apply to your sale. A delayed parcel, a confirmed lost shipment and an order marked delivered are three different situations.

Here's how to handle each one, including what Depop's published guidance says about refunds and seller protection.

What Should You Do If a Depop Package Is Lost?

Before assuming the money is gone, work through these steps.

1. Check the tracking status

Open the sold order and check the tracking information. If a tracking number is available, check it on the carrier's own website too. Look at the latest scan and when it occurred.

  • Still in transit: The parcel may still be moving through the carrier's network.
  • No recent updates: The carrier may not have scanned the parcel recently, or the tracking information may not have updated.
  • Marked delivered: The carrier reports delivery, but the buyer says the package is missing.
  • Confirmed lost: The carrier or Depop has confirmed that the parcel cannot be located.

Don't treat these outcomes as interchangeable. A quiet tracking page doesn't prove a package is lost, and a delivery scan creates a different problem from a parcel that never reached its destination.

2. Keep your shipping evidence

Find your original postage receipt, shipping label and tracking number. Save the tracking history and any available proof of dispatch or delivery. Keep the Depop order details and messages with the buyer too.

If the carrier opens an investigation, save the case number and any written response. If Depop asks for proof of shipping, you'll be able to provide the records rather than trying to reconstruct what happened.

Depop also recommends using tracked shipping and keeping a copy of your original proof of postage or shipping receipt in its seller safety guidance.

3. Contact the carrier when necessary

If tracking has stopped updating or the carrier reports a delivery problem, ask the carrier what it can establish about the shipment. For a missing parcel, ask whether an investigation can be opened and whether the carrier considers the shipment delayed, lost or delivered.

Keep the response. A carrier investigation does not guarantee compensation, but its findings may help you understand the situation and respond to Depop.

4. Keep the buyer informed

You don't need to accept blame for something you haven't established happened. You should still communicate clearly.

Hi! I'm sorry your package hasn't arrived. I've checked the tracking and am looking into the latest update. I'll keep the shipping details and any investigation information together and follow up through Depop if needed.

Keep the conversation factual and, where possible, within Depop. Don't promise a refund or claim that the carrier has lost the parcel before you know what happened. If the buyer opens a case, follow the instructions in that case and respond within the stated timeframe.

Does Depop Cover Lost Packages for Sellers?

Depop Seller Protection may cover an eligible lost shipment, but not every sale qualifies. The outcome depends in part on how you shipped the order and whether you meet the applicable requirements.

Depop's official guide for sellers whose buyers haven't received their orders gives different instructions for Depop Shipping and independent shipping.

If you used Depop Shipping

According to Depop's published guidance:

  1. Check the tracking information.
  2. If tracking hasn't updated 10 days after shipping, contact Depop Support.
  3. If Depop confirms the parcel has been lost or damaged in transit, Depop says it will refund the buyer on your behalf and compensate you under Depop Protection, up to the applicable limit.

That last step matters. The policy describes a process following confirmation of loss or damage; it doesn't promise compensation for every delayed shipment or every dispute.

Who qualifies for Depop Seller Protection?

Depop's Seller Protection policy states that protection is available to eligible sellers in the US, UK and Australia who use a Depop Shipping label.

RequirementWhat it means for you
Eligible saleThe item must be sold through Depop using the BUY button and must not be prohibited.
Correct shipping methodYou must use the Depop Shipping label generated for the order.
Dispatch deadlineThe item must be sent within five days of the transaction date.
Correct addressThe item must go to the buyer's address shown on the sales receipt.
Value limitThe transaction must be within the applicable protection limit.
Respond to DepopYou must answer communications and provide requested information within the specified timeframe.

The policy lists maximum eligible transaction values of:

  • US: $300
  • UK: £250
  • Australia: A$450

These limits include the item price, shipping fees and applicable taxes. Bundle sales count as one transaction for the limit, not as separate limits for every item. All relevant eligibility requirements must be met.

What if you shipped the order yourself?

This is where sellers need to be especially careful. Depop's published guidance says that if you shipped independently, you need to keep the buyer informed. If the package is delayed, tell the buyer about the updated estimated delivery date where available.

If the package is lost in transit, Depop's guidance directs the seller to start a compensation claim with the carrier to seek reimbursement for shipping costs and to refund the buyer in full. It also states that independent shipping is not covered by Depop Seller Protection.

That means independent tracking or a postage receipt is useful evidence, but it does not give you the same protection as an eligible Depop Shipping label. If you've already shipped independently and a package goes missing, check the applicable rules and follow the instructions for your transaction.

What If Tracking Says Delivered but the Buyer Says They Never Received It?

This is one of the most frustrating problems for a seller. Your tracking says delivered. The buyer says there is no package. You may have done everything correctly, but you still need to deal with the claim.

Check the delivery details first

Review the carrier's tracking page. Check the delivery date, the address associated with the shipment and any available delivery notes or proof of delivery. Ask the buyer to check the usual delivery locations, such as a parcel locker, building reception, designated safe place or with a neighbour. If the delivery appears incorrect, contact the carrier and ask it to investigate.

Does a delivery scan guarantee that the seller wins?

No. Depop's Buyer Protection policy says that a refund is not guaranteed when tracked shipping shows that a lost item was delivered to the buyer's address.

But that does not mean every dispute will automatically be decided in the seller's favour. The outcome depends on the circumstances and the applicable policy. If Depop contacts you, provide the tracking information and any other evidence requested. Be accurate about what the tracking proves and what remains uncertain.

What if the buyer says the package was stolen?

A package reported as stolen after delivery is different from one the carrier confirms was lost in transit. Keep the original tracking evidence, communicate with the buyer and follow any Depop case instructions. The buyer may need to check with the carrier or take other appropriate steps.

Don't promise the buyer that Depop will refund them. Equally, don't assume that a delivery scan automatically ends every possible dispute. Your job is to provide the genuine evidence available and follow the process for the order.

What If You Shipped Without Tracking?

If you sent an order using a method that doesn't provide meaningful tracking, it may be harder to establish what happened after dispatch.

Start by gathering whatever evidence you have:

  • Your postage or shipping receipt.
  • The date you handed the package to the carrier.
  • The destination recorded on the shipping documentation.
  • Any carrier reference or dispatch confirmation.
  • The Depop order details and relevant buyer messages.

Proof of postage can help establish that you handed a parcel to the carrier. It does not necessarily establish that the buyer received it.

If a claim has already been raised, provide the genuine evidence you have and follow Depop's instructions. Don't invent a tracking number or claim that an item was delivered if you cannot establish that it was.

For future orders, consider using a tracked shipping method and check which shipping arrangements qualify for protection in your country. Depop recommends tracked shipping and keeping proof of postage in its seller safety guidance.

Can a Lost Depop Package Cost the Seller Money?

Yes. A missing shipment can create a financial risk, but the amount you ultimately lose depends on what happens to the order and whether any protection or carrier compensation applies.

Consider this example. You sell a jacket for $60, and the buyer pays $7 for shipping. The carrier later confirms that the package is lost.

ItemAmount
Item sale price$60
Buyer-paid shipping$7
Original order value$67

The $67 is the original order value, not your net loss or guaranteed reimbursement.

Your actual financial position depends on several things: what you paid for the original shipping, whether you need to refund the buyer, whether Depop Seller Protection applies, whether the carrier offers compensation, and whether the item itself can be recovered.

For example, an eligible Depop Shipping order may qualify for protection following confirmation of loss. An independently shipped order is not covered by Depop Seller Protection under the cited guidance, and Depop directs the seller to pursue a carrier claim and refund the buyer.

What if the buyer requests a refund?

Don't ignore the request or assume you must immediately pay without checking what happened. Review the tracking, gather your shipping evidence and follow any open Depop case. If Depop's process requires a refund, follow the instructions you receive.

For independent shipping, Depop's published guidance specifically says that if the parcel is lost in transit, the seller must refund the buyer in full and pursue compensation from the carrier for shipping costs.

If you're trying to understand the fee consequences of a refund, read our Depop fees refunded guide. For general labels, costs and shipping methods, see Depop Shipping Explained.

What If the Buyer Opens a Depop Dispute?

If the buyer raises an issue, don't ignore it—even if your tracking shows that you shipped the order. Read the case instructions carefully and respond within the timeframe Depop gives you.

What evidence should you provide?

Depending on the situation, useful records may include:

  • The order and shipping details.
  • The tracking number and tracking history.
  • Your postage receipt or proof of dispatch.
  • The address recorded on the order.
  • Any carrier investigation reference or written response.
  • Relevant messages between you and the buyer.

Provide only genuine, relevant evidence. If Depop requests more information, answer the questions it asks. Keep copies of what you submit and the replies you receive.

What if you cannot prove that you shipped the item?

This can make resolving the dispute more difficult. Depop's guidance for sellers whose buyers haven't received their orders says sellers should respond promptly and provide proof of shipping when requested. It also warns that a buyer may be automatically refunded if the seller does not respond or cannot prove shipment.

That is why keeping your original receipt and tracking details matters. Don't wait until a dispute opens to start looking for them.

How to Reduce the Risk of Losing Money on Future Depop Orders

You cannot prevent every carrier problem. You can make a future dispute easier to handle.

Before shipping:

  • Check which shipping method qualifies for protection in your country.
  • Use the correct label and the address shown on the order.
  • Dispatch within the applicable deadline.
  • Keep your receipt and tracking details.

After shipping:

  • Check that tracking information is available.
  • Save the order and shipping records.
  • Tell the buyer about significant delays.
  • Respond to carrier investigations and Depop requests promptly.
  • Keep all relevant messages and evidence until the issue is resolved.

The most useful habit is to retain proof of what actually happened. If something goes wrong, you should be able to establish the dispatch date, shipping method, destination and tracking status without relying on memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Depop sellers responsible for lost packages?

It depends on the circumstances and the shipping method. Depop's guidance provides a protection process for eligible Depop Shipping orders. For independently shipped orders confirmed lost in transit, its guidance directs the seller to refund the buyer in full and pursue a carrier compensation claim for shipping costs.

Does Depop cover lost packages for sellers?

Eligible sellers in the US, UK and Australia may qualify for Depop Seller Protection if they meet all the applicable conditions, including using a Depop Shipping label, dispatching within five days and shipping to the address on the sales receipt. Coverage is not guaranteed.

What should I do if my Depop package is lost?

Check the tracking, save your shipping evidence and contact the carrier when appropriate. If you used Depop Shipping and tracking hasn't updated for 10 days after shipping, Depop says to contact Support. If you shipped independently, follow the carrier's missing-parcel and compensation process and keep the buyer informed.

What happens if tracking says delivered but the buyer says the package never arrived?

Keep the tracking evidence, review the delivery details and follow the carrier's investigation process if necessary. Depop's Buyer Protection policy says a refund is not guaranteed when tracking shows delivery to the buyer's address. A delivery scan does not guarantee a particular outcome in every dispute.

Does Depop protect sellers who ship independently?

Depop's published Seller Protection policy excludes independently shipped orders for sellers in the US, UK and Australia. Sellers based outside those countries are also ineligible for this programme. If you ship independently and a parcel is confirmed lost, Depop's guidance directs you to refund the buyer in full and pursue a carrier compensation claim for shipping costs.

Will Depop refund a buyer if a package is stolen?

A refund is not guaranteed simply because a buyer reports a stolen package. Depop's Buyer Protection policy says it cannot guarantee a refund when tracking shows that the parcel was delivered to the buyer's address. The circumstances and applicable rules matter.

Can a buyer open a dispute if I have tracking?

Tracking does not prevent a buyer from reporting a missing order. If Depop opens a case or requests information, respond promptly and provide the genuine shipping and delivery evidence available to you.

Can I get compensation from the shipping carrier?

Possibly. It depends on the carrier, the shipping service and the circumstances of the loss. Check the carrier's claim process and any applicable deadlines. Carrier compensation is separate from Depop Seller Protection.

Does Depop Seller Protection guarantee reimbursement?

No. Depop's published terms state that Seller Protection is not insurance or a guarantee. A transaction must meet the applicable eligibility conditions, and Depop decides whether protection is granted under its policy.

Final Thoughts

A missing Depop package is stressful enough without having to guess what happens to your money.

Start with the tracking, keep your shipping evidence, communicate clearly and follow the appropriate process. Then check whether your shipping method and transaction qualify for Depop Seller Protection.

If tracking says delivered, don't assume the case is automatically closed in your favour. If the parcel is confirmed lost, don't assume every shipment qualifies for reimbursement either.

Find out what happened, keep your evidence and make decisions based on the applicable policy—not assumptions.

Policy note: This article references Depop's published Seller Protection, Buyer Protection and missing-order guidance. Policies can change, so check the current official Help Centre and the instructions for your specific transaction before taking action.
