Vinted Returns for Sellers: What Can You Actually Lose?

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

Vinted's no-selling-fee model is a real advantage: you generally don't pay a standard fee just to list or sell an item. But no seller fee doesn't mean no seller loss.

A sale can still go wrong. A parcel may be lost or damaged, a buyer may report that an item is significantly not as described, or a returned item may arrive in a different condition. You might also agree to cover return shipping or accept a partial refund.

When that happens, the useful question isn't only "Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?" It's: "After this transaction goes wrong, what do I still have — and what have I lost?"

This guide explains the main Vinted return scenarios, the evidence sellers should keep, and how to think about the financial impact. Vinted's rules and procedures can change, so check the current policy for your country and the specific order before acting.

Vinted has no standard selling fee — but sellers can still lose money

Vinted says there are no fees for uploading or selling items. That means sellers generally don't lose a standard commission from the selling price.

However, a problem transaction can still involve:

  • a refund or partial refund;
  • return shipping that the seller agrees to cover;
  • a parcel that is lost or damaged;
  • an item returned in worse condition or with parts missing;
  • packaging or shipping problems; or
  • a lower resale value after the item comes back.

Keep two questions separate:

  • Selling fee: What does Vinted charge for the sale?
  • Financial loss: How much worse off are you after the transaction is resolved?

They are not the same thing.

When can a Vinted buyer report a problem?

Vinted's refund process can apply to eligible situations such as an order that was not delivered or is confirmed lost, an item that arrived damaged, or an item that is significantly not as described (often shortened to SNAD). A buyer simply changing their mind is not automatically the same as a valid SNAD claim.

The exact outcome depends on the facts, the applicable policy, and the evidence available.

If the parcel is lost

Do not assume that every lost parcel automatically becomes a personal loss for the seller. Vinted's process distinguishes confirmed lost shipments from other problems, and the outcome can depend on the shipping method and circumstances.

Keep tracking details, proof of drop-off, and any relevant carrier messages. If tracking has stopped updating, use the order's Vinted process rather than relying only on an informal conversation.

If the item arrives damaged

Try to distinguish between an item that was already damaged before shipping and one that may have been damaged in transit. Those are different situations, and photos taken before dispatch can help document the item's original condition.

If the item is significantly not as described

Read the buyer's exact complaint and compare it with your listing and photographs. A minor difference is not automatically the same as a significant mismatch. Equally, don't dismiss a genuine issue just because the item looked fine when you packed it.

Who pays return shipping on Vinted?

Under Vinted's general refund guidance, the buyer generally pays return shipping, but a seller may choose to cover it — for example, by providing a Vinted-generated return label. Check the policy and options shown for the specific order before agreeing to a resolution.

That means a return does not automatically mean the seller pays postage. But if you agree to cover the return, that cost becomes part of the transaction's financial result.

Can a Vinted seller offer a partial refund?

A partial refund may be available as part of an agreed resolution in applicable circumstances. In that situation, the buyer may keep the item while receiving some money back.

That is financially different from a full refund with a return. When you assess the result, identify what actually happened:

  1. Full refund and item returned
  2. Full refund without the item being returned
  3. Partial refund while the buyer keeps the item

Don't label every problem a "return." The resolution determines what value you recover and what costs remain.

What should you do if a Vinted return comes back damaged?

This is a stressful situation: you shipped an item in one condition, accepted a return, and now it appears to have come back damaged, altered, or incomplete.

Act promptly. Vinted's current terms describe a limited period — stated as 2 days after notification that the return should have arrived — for the seller to confirm that the return is okay or report a problem. Check the exact deadline displayed for your order and your country.

Before you confirm that everything is fine:

  1. Photograph the outer package and shipping label.
  2. Keep the packaging and any protective material.
  3. Photograph the item from several angles.
  4. Take close-ups of damage, alterations, or missing parts.
  5. Compare the returned item with your original listing and pre-shipping photos.
  6. Report the issue through Vinted's order process within the applicable deadline.

Don't rely on memory, and don't throw the packaging away while the issue is unresolved.

What if the buyer sends back the wrong item?

If the returned item appears different from the one you sent, document the discrepancy immediately. Useful evidence may include:

  • original listing photos;
  • photos taken immediately before shipping;
  • unique marks, labels, or serial numbers where applicable;
  • photos of the return package and label;
  • tracking information; and
  • photos of the returned item and any missing components.

Report the concern through Vinted's process. Avoid making accusations you cannot support; describe the visible differences and provide the evidence you have.

What if the buyer says the item was damaged in transit?

There are several possibilities: the item may have been damaged before shipping, damaged in transit, or the parties may disagree about when the damage occurred. Photos of the item and packed parcel can help establish what the package looked like before dispatch.

You do not need to create a full photo shoot for every low-value sale. For higher-value or fragile items, a quick record of the item's condition, protective packaging, sealed parcel, and shipping label can be worthwhile.

A simple sequence is:

Item condition→protective packaging→sealed parcel→shipping label

These photos do not guarantee a particular decision, but they can make it easier to explain what happened.

What does SNAD mean on Vinted?

SNAD means "Significantly Not As Described." It refers to a significant difference between the listing and the item received — not simply that the buyer no longer wants it.

Examples can include a materially different size or colour, serious undisclosed damage, holes, stains, missing components, or an item that is materially different from what was represented. Check Vinted's current policy for the exact criteria and exclusions.

For sellers, the practical lesson is to make listings accurate. Mention meaningful wear, stains, missing accessories, scratches, fading, altered measurements, or other defects that a buyer would reasonably care about. Clear descriptions cannot prevent every dispute, but they can reduce avoidable misunderstandings.

Can a buyer return an item just because they changed their mind?

Under Vinted's standard refund policy, an item that matches its description but simply isn't wanted, doesn't fit, or doesn't suit the buyer is not automatically eligible for a refund. The buyer may ask whether you are willing to accept a return, but that is different from an eligible SNAD claim.

Always check the policy that applies to your country and transaction. Don't assume that "I don't want it anymore" automatically means you must accept a return — or that every complaint is invalid.

What if the buyer used or damaged the item after delivery?

Vinted's refund guidance distinguishes an eligible problem with an item from an item that has been extensively used or damaged after successful delivery. If you believe the item has materially changed, document the condition and raise the concern through Vinted's process.

Keep the discussion factual: explain what differs from the original listing or photos, and provide clear evidence.

What happens if a Vinted seller doesn't ship?

Vinted's selling guidance generally gives sellers a limited shipping window — often stated as 5 business days after purchase, unless an extension is agreed. If the order is not shipped within the applicable period, it may be cancelled.

That is not a conventional return, but it is still a failed transaction: the seller never reaches a completed sale. Check the shipping deadline shown for the order, and ship promptly with the correct method.

Does Vinted pay a seller if a parcel is lost?

There isn't a safe one-word answer for every situation. Vinted's process distinguishes confirmed lost or damaged shipments from other cases, and the seller-side outcome can depend on the shipping method, tracking, circumstances, and Vinted's decision.

Keep:

  • tracking information;
  • proof of drop-off where available;
  • shipping details; and
  • relevant carrier updates.

Use the order's official Vinted process to report a missing parcel and follow the instructions shown for that transaction.

What happens while a Vinted order is suspended?

When a buyer reports an issue within the applicable period, the order may be suspended while Vinted's process reviews the problem. That does not necessarily mean the money has already been permanently taken from the seller. The eventual outcome depends on how the case is resolved.

Read the notice in the order carefully and respond within the stated timeframe.

How long does a Vinted buyer have to report a problem or return an item?

Vinted's policy has specific time limits. The policy guidance referenced for this guide states that buyers generally have 2 calendar days after Vinted's delivery or lost-order notification to report an issue, and that a required return is generally to be shipped within 5 business days, unless otherwise agreed. It also describes a 2-day period for the seller to confirm a returned item is okay or report a problem after notification that it should have arrived.

These are important deadlines, but don't rely on a general article instead of the live order instructions. Check the deadline and policy shown in your account, because procedures can change and may vary by country or shipping method.

Vinted returns and refunds are not the same thing

A return describes an item being sent back. A refund describes money being returned to the buyer. They do not always happen together.

Common outcomes include:

  • full refund with the item returned;
  • full refund without a return, where the applicable process allows it; and
  • partial refund while the buyer keeps the item.

For your own records, note the actual resolution, the shipping costs you paid, and the condition and resale value of any item you received back.

What can a Vinted return actually cost you?

Imagine you sold an item for $50. Vinted did not charge you a standard selling fee. A problem then leads to a refund, you agree to pay $6 for return shipping, and the item comes back damaged. You estimate its realistic resale value is now $30.

The important question is not simply "Was the $50 refunded?" You need to account for the item's remaining value and any additional costs. A refund amount and a seller's economic loss are not necessarily the same number.

For example, if the item is back in your possession and can still be resold for $30, that remaining value matters. The $6 return-shipping cost also matters. Other costs — such as original outbound shipping or packaging — should only be included if they were actually borne by you and are relevant to the scenario.

Avoid a simplistic sale-price-minus-refund calculation. Look at what money left, what costs you paid, and what value you still have.

A practical checklist before shipping a valuable Vinted item

Condition

  • Photograph the item and its overall condition.
  • Photograph existing defects.
  • Photograph identifying details where relevant.

Listing

  • Make sure the description matches the actual item.
  • Mention meaningful defects and missing accessories.
  • Include useful measurements or details when relevant.

Packaging

  • Use appropriate protection.
  • Photograph the item packed safely.
  • Photograph the sealed parcel.

Shipping

  • Keep tracking information and drop-off evidence where available.
  • Follow the shipping method and deadline shown in the order.
  • Keep relevant carrier records.

If a buyer reports a problem

  • Read the exact complaint.
  • Compare it with the original listing and photos.
  • Respond through Vinted's process.
  • Keep the evidence together and watch the deadline.

The goal isn't to treat every buyer as a potential problem. It's to avoid being left with no evidence when something genuinely goes wrong.

What seller discussions can — and cannot — tell you

Seller and buyer discussions can reveal recurring worries: damaged parcels, missing components, disagreements about condition, and uncertainty about what evidence a platform will request. But individual posts are anecdotes, not official policy and not proof of how Vinted will decide another case.

The practical lesson is straightforward: document the item and package, follow the official process, and avoid assuming that one person's outcome guarantees yours.

What Vinted sellers should not assume

  • "There are no seller fees, so I can't lose money."
    A transaction can still involve refunds, shipping costs, a damaged item, or reduced resale value.
  • "Every return means I pay return postage."
    Not necessarily. Vinted's general policy says buyers generally pay return shipping, although sellers can choose to cover it.
  • "The buyer says it is damaged, so I automatically lose."
    The facts, evidence, and applicable process matter.
  • "Once an item is returned, the case is finished."
    The returned item's condition can itself become an issue.
  • "A buyer can always return something they don't like."
    A change of mind is not automatically the same as an eligible SNAD claim under Vinted's standard refund policy.
  • "I can wait several days before checking a return."
    That can be risky when the applicable process gives you a short window to report a problem.

The bottom line: Vinted is fee-free, not risk-free

Vinted's no-selling-fee model can help sellers keep more of a successful sale. But it does not remove every risk from a problem transaction.

When something goes wrong, work through the actual sequence:

What did the buyer report?→Which policy applies?→What resolution occurred?→Who paid return shipping?→What condition is the item in now?→What evidence do you have?→What value do you still have?

That is how you move from "Vinted charged me no selling fee" to understanding your actual transaction loss.

Vinted Returns FAQ

Does Vinted charge sellers a selling fee?

Vinted says there are no fees for uploading or selling items under its stated model. Check the current terms for your country and any optional services.

Who pays return shipping on Vinted?

Buyers generally pay return shipping under Vinted's general refund policy, but a seller may choose to cover it. Check the instructions for the specific order.

Can a Vinted buyer return an item because they don't like it?

Not automatically under the standard refund policy if the item matches its description. A buyer can ask whether the seller is willing to accept a return.

What does SNAD mean on Vinted?

SNAD means "Significantly Not As Described." It concerns a significant difference between the listing and the item received.

What if a Vinted return comes back damaged?

Photograph the package, label, packaging, and item; preserve the evidence; and report the issue through Vinted within the deadline shown for your order.

What if the buyer returns the wrong item?

Document the differences and report the issue through Vinted. Original listing photos, identifying details, and return-package evidence may help.

Can a Vinted seller offer a partial refund?

A partial refund may be available as part of an agreed resolution in applicable circumstances. Check the options shown for your order.

How long does a buyer have to report a problem?

Vinted's policy guidance states that buyers generally have 2 calendar days after the relevant delivery or lost-order notification. Confirm the live order instructions for the applicable deadline.

How long does a buyer have to return an item?

Vinted's policy guidance states that a required return is generally to be shipped within 5 business days unless otherwise agreed. Check the instructions for your order.

Can sellers lose money even though Vinted has no selling fee?

Yes. Depending on the circumstances, a seller may face return-shipping costs, a lost or damaged item, a refund-related loss, or reduced resale value.
