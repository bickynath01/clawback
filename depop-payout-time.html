When Do You Get Paid on Depop? Payout Timing & Pending Money Explained (2026)

Quick Answer: When Do You Get Paid on Depop?

There is not one universal Depop payout timeline. Depop publishes different timing depending on your market and, in some markets, your shipping method.

  • US: Depop sends payment to your Depop Balance 2 business days after the item shows as delivered or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first.
  • UK: With Depop Shipping via Evri, payment is sent 2 business days after tracking shows delivered or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first. If you ship your own way, payment is sent 10 business days after delivery.
  • Australia: Depop's detailed payout guidance says Depop Shipping or tracked AusPost can pay 2–3 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first. Other carriers use 10 business days after the sale.

After Depop sends the payment, your bank can take additional time to post it. Use the tracker below to estimate the payout date based on the rule that applies to you — not as a guaranteed bank-deposit date.

You did everything right. You photographed the item, priced it, packed it securely and dropped it in the mail. Then the tracking changes to "Delivered." You open Depop expecting to see the money ready to withdraw — but your balance still says Pending, or the payout has not reached your bank.

If you've ever searched "when do I get paid from Depop?", "Depop says delivered but no money" or "why is my Depop payout delayed?", the confusing part is that there isn't one worldwide payout rule. Depop's official Help Centre gives different timing for the US, UK and Australia, and the UK and Australia rules can change depending on how the order was shipped. This guide turns those rules into plain English and gives you a practical payout-window estimator.

The goal is simple: figure out when Depop should send the payment, understand why your balance might still say Pending or On hold, and know what to check if the expected payout date has passed.

What Time Does Depop Release Funds?

Depop does not publish a universal clock time such as "payouts arrive between 9am and noon." So if you're waiting for money today, don't use a seller-reported time as an official deadline.

The useful information is the scheduled payout date and the status in your Depop Balance. If the payment has been sent, your bank may still take additional time to show it. If the scheduled date has passed, check your payment details and the status of the payout before contacting support.

When Will Depop Pay Me? (Does Depop Pay Immediately After Delivery?)

Not necessarily. Depop's published rules do not say that money reaches your bank immediately after the carrier marks an item delivered. In the US, for example, Depop says the payment is sent 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.

That distinction matters: sale → delivery → Depop sends payment → bank posts payment are separate steps. If you are planning inventory purchases around a payout, use the published payout schedule and leave room for bank processing rather than assuming "Delivered" means "cash today."

Why Is My Depop Payout Delayed? (What to Check)

If the expected payout date has passed, don't immediately assume Depop has placed a secret seller hold on your account. Start with the status shown in your Depop Balance and work through the concrete checks below.

1. The published payout window has not finished

Compare your sale date and delivery date with the rule for your country and shipping method. The US and UK Depop Shipping rules use delivery timing or a sale-date maximum, while other shipping setups can use a different rule.

2. Your payment is still Pending

Depop says a Pending payout means the buyer has paid but your funds are waiting to clear. In the US, Depop specifically lists an item that has not yet been delivered as one reason a payment can remain pending.

3. Your payment is On hold because of an issue

Depop says an On hold payout means the buyer has raised an issue with the purchase and your funds are being held until the issue is resolved. That is different from a normal payout simply waiting for its scheduled date.

4. Your bank or account information needs attention

Depop's official payout guidance says missing or incorrect information can affect payouts. Check that the correct bank account is connected and that your account is verified where required. In Australia, Depop also lists incorrect account details, unsupported or restricted bank accounts, closed accounts and currency mismatches among possible failed-payout causes.

5. Your Depop Balance is negative

Depop says a negative balance can happen when a refund is issued while there isn't enough money available. New payments can be used to clear the negative balance. If you see a negative balance, check the Balance activity rather than treating the missing payout as a normal timing delay.

What we removed from older versions of this guide: Depop's current payout pages do not establish a universal 21–30-day "new seller rolling reserve," a universal $500 security hold, or an official 9am–12pm payout window. We don't present those claims as Depop policy without a direct current source.

Does Depop Pay on Weekends or Holidays?

Depop's payout pages express the timing in business days, and the bank can take additional time after the payment is sent. Depop does not publish a universal "weekend payout" promise on the payout pages used for this guide, so don't rely on a specific Saturday, Sunday or Monday arrival time.

If the payout date falls around a bank holiday or weekend, allow for the fact that bank processing may not follow ordinary calendar-day counting. The safest approach is to use the payout date shown by Depop and allow additional bank-processing time.

How to Avoid Unnecessary Depop Payout Delays

You cannot change Depop's published payout schedule, but you can reduce avoidable problems that stop a payment from reaching the right account.

  1. Ship promptly. Delivery timing is part of the payout rule in the US, UK Depop Shipping and some Australia shipping setups.
  2. Keep tracking information accurate. If you ship your own way, make sure the relevant tracking information is entered correctly so the order can show its delivery status.
  3. Keep your bank details current. Depop specifically tells sellers to check their connected bank account and payment settings when a payout has not arrived.
  4. Complete account verification when required. Depop's Australia payout guidance says payouts cannot be received until the account is verified and a bank account is connected.
  5. Watch for Pending or On hold. These statuses tell you that the problem may be the payment clearing or an open buyer issue rather than ordinary bank processing.

Don't rely on buyer ratings to trigger payment. Depop's official payout rules are based on its published payout schedule and payment status, not on a buyer leaving a five-star review.

The Tax Reality of Depop Payouts

Payout timing and tax reporting are separate questions. The date Depop sends money to your bank does not, by itself, answer how the sale should be treated for tax purposes. Keep your sale records and use current tax guidance for the rules that apply to you.

Depop Says Delivered but I Haven't Been Paid: What Should I Do?

This is one of the most common payout questions because the tracking page and the payment timeline are not the same thing. A carrier scan saying Delivered tells Depop that the package reached its destination; it does not mean your bank account must show the money immediately.

Start by checking these five things in order:

  1. Check the sale date. Some Depop payout rules use a maximum number of business days from the sale, so the sale date can matter even when the order has already been delivered.
  2. Check the delivery scan. If your market uses delivery-based timing, make sure the tracking actually shows Delivered rather than In Transit, Label Created or another earlier status.
  3. Check Depop Balance. If the payment is Pending, your money is still waiting to clear. If it is On hold, Depop says a buyer has raised an issue.
  4. Check your bank details and verification. A correct payout schedule cannot help if the connected bank account or required verification information is incomplete or incorrect.
  5. Check whether the expected payout date has passed. If it has, use the transaction details in Depop Balance and contact Depop Support if the account information is correct.
Don't confuse "payout sent" with "payout received." Depop's published pages say the bank can take additional time after the payment is sent. That is why a seller can have a payout that is no longer Pending while the money is still not visible in the bank account.

What Does Pending Mean on Depop?

Pending is not the same as a failed payout. Depop says a Pending payout means the buyer has paid but the funds are waiting to clear. In the US guidance, Depop specifically gives an undelivered item as an example of why a payment can remain pending.

For a seller, the practical question is therefore not simply "How many days has it been?" It is:

  • Has the order been delivered?
  • What country and shipping method apply?
  • What payout date does Depop show?
  • Is the transaction Pending or On hold?
  • Is there a problem with your bank or account verification?

Those answers tell you whether you are looking at normal payout timing or a payout that needs troubleshooting.

What Does On Hold Mean on Depop?

On hold is different from Pending. Depop says an On hold payout means the buyer has raised an issue with the purchase and your funds are being held until that issue is resolved.

That distinction matters because a seller can spend hours calculating a payout date when the real reason the money has not arrived is an open buyer issue. If your transaction is On hold, focus on the issue shown in Depop rather than treating the normal payout schedule as a countdown that will override the dispute.

Seller warning: Never tell a seller that a normal payout date guarantees payment when an order is On hold. The dispute status changes the situation.

What If My Depop Payout Failed?

A failed payout is a different problem from a payout that is simply waiting for its scheduled date. Depop's Australia payout guidance gives several examples of information that can cause a payout to fail, including incorrect bank details, an incorrect account number or account-holder name, a bank that no longer supports payouts, a currency mismatch, a restricted account or a closed account.

If a payout fails, check the transaction inside your Depop Balance/Payouts area. Depop says the failed payout can show information about what needs to be resolved. Correct the account information and follow Depop's support instructions if the problem continues.

What If My Depop Balance Is Negative?

A negative balance can be especially confusing because a seller may think, "I made a sale, so why can't I withdraw it?" Depop says a negative balance can occur when a refund is issued while there is not enough money available in the Depop Balance.

That means a new sale does not necessarily translate into a new bank deposit immediately. Depop's Balance guidance says new payments can be used to cancel out the negative balance. In applicable situations, Depop may also automatically top up the balance through the connected payment method.

If your balance is negative, treat the negative amount as part of your current cash position. The important number is the available balance after the adjustment, not simply the gross amount of your latest sale.

How Depop Payout Timing Affects Reseller Cash Flow

For casual sellers, waiting a few business days may not matter. For a reseller who uses every sale to buy the next piece of inventory, it can matter a lot.

Imagine you sell five items during a week and plan to use the proceeds to source another batch. If those sales are still moving through the payout process, your sales revenue and your available cash are not the same number.

Conclusion: Stop Refreshing, Start Tracking

The anxiety is understandable: "Delivered" looks like the finish line, but it isn't necessarily the moment the money reaches your bank. The safest way to track a Depop payout is to start with the rule for your country and shipping method, then check whether the payment is Pending, On hold, Available or affected by a negative balance.

Most importantly, don't let an old generic "2–10 days" article or an unsupported new-seller hold estimate dictate your cash flow. Use the current Depop rule, use the payout date shown in your account where available, and allow additional time for your bank to post the payment.
