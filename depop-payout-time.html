When Do You Get Paid on Depop? The 2-10 Day Rule Explained

Quick Answer: When Do You Get Paid on Depop?

Depop pays you 2 to 10 working days after delivery (once tracking shows "Delivered"). New sellers face holds of 21 to 30 calendar days. Bank transfers add 1-3 business days on top.

Total timeline:

  • Established sellers: 4-14 days from delivery to bank
  • New sellers: 25-35 days from delivery to bank

What Time Does Depop Release Funds?

This is one of the most common questions in Depop seller communities: "What time does Depop pay out today?" The honest answer is that Depop doesn't publish an exact time. However, many sellers report payouts hitting their bank accounts between 9am and 12pm local time on standard banking days.

If your funds are released on a Friday afternoon, the actual bank transfer might not initiate until Monday morning, meaning you won't see the money until Tuesday or Wednesday. Depop payouts are processed through standard banking networks (ACH in the US, BACS in the UK), which do not operate on weekends or bank holidays. Always calculate your cash flow around business days, not calendar days.

When Will Depop Pay Me? (Does Depop Pay Immediately After Delivery?)

No. Depop never releases funds immediately, even for established sellers with thousands of 5-star reviews. The platform operates on an escrow system, which means your money is held in a secure vault until the buyer has had adequate time to inspect the item. The minimum waiting period is 2 working days after delivery, even in the best-case scenario.

If you were hoping to list an item, sell it, and withdraw the cash the same day — Depop is not that platform. If you need instant payouts, you're better off selling on eBay (for established sellers) or using local marketplaces like Facebook Marketplace.

Why Is My Depop Payout Delayed? (The 4 Red Flags)

If it's been over 10 days since delivery and your Depop funds are still pending or on hold, one of these four things is happening. Don't panic — most of these are automatic security measures, not something you did wrong.

1. You forgot to add the tracking number

This is the #1 reason for delayed payouts. If you bought a label outside of Depop (like at the post office) and just mailed it without typing the tracking number into the app, Depop has no idea the item was delivered. Without a "Delivered" scan from an integrated carrier, Depop will hold the funds indefinitely or until the buyer manually confirms receipt. Always, always upload the tracking number.

2. The buyer opened a dispute or requested a refund

If the buyer claims the item never arrived, or they open a case saying it's not as described, Depop immediately freezes the payout. The funds will not be released until the dispute is resolved. This is why photographing your items before shipping is non-negotiable. Our return loss calculator can help you quantify exactly what a dispute costs you.

3. You are a brand-new seller (The 21–30 Day Hold)

When you first open a Depop shop, you are a massive fraud risk in their eyes. Scammers frequently create new accounts, sell fake designer goods, take the money, and vanish. To combat this, Depop places a new seller rolling reserve on your account. They may hold your funds for 21 to 30 days to ensure you are a legitimate seller shipping real items. As you build up 5-star reviews, this hold shrinks and eventually disappears.

Relief note for new sellers: this isn't personal. Every single reseller on Depop went through this exact holding pattern. The 21–30 day window feels like forever when you're first starting out, but as you build a track record of clean sales and 5-star reviews, the hold shrinks and eventually disappears. The first month is just tuition. Push through it and the payouts normalize permanently. While you wait, learn exactly what Depop takes in fees so you can price your items correctly from day one.

4. You had a sudden spike in sales

If you normally sell two items a week, and suddenly you sell $2,000 worth of vintage denim in a single weekend, Depop's automated security bots will flag your account. A sudden spike looks like a hacked account or a scammer cashing out. They will temporarily pause your payouts while they manually review your account. If this happens, just verify your identity in the app settings and the hold will lift.

Does Depop Pay on Weekends or Holidays?

No. Depop payouts are processed through standard banking networks (like ACH in the US or BACS in the UK). These networks do not operate on weekends or bank holidays. If your funds are released on a Friday afternoon, the actual bank transfer might not initiate until Monday morning, meaning you won't see the money until Tuesday or Wednesday. Always calculate your cash flow around business days, not calendar days.

How to Get Paid Faster on Depop (Pro Tips)

You can't change Depop's core rules, but you can avoid the delays that sellers accidentally trigger. Do these three things on every single sale:

  1. Ship within 24 hours. The faster it gets marked "Delivered," the faster the 2-to-10-day clock starts.
  2. Use Depop's integrated shipping or upload tracking immediately. Never mail an item without the tracking number being inside the app. No tracking = no payout.
  3. Message the buyer on delivery day. Send a quick, polite message: "Hi! Tracking says delivered. Hope you love it! If everything is great, I'd so appreciate a 5-star rating." When a buyer leaves a 5-star rating, it signals to Depop that the transaction is complete, which can speed up the release of funds.

The Tax Reality of Depop Payouts

Just because Depop holds your money for 10 days doesn't mean it's not "yours" for tax purposes. In the eyes of the IRS (and most tax authorities), you earned the income the moment the sale was made, not the moment the money hit your bank account. When you are tracking your yearly revenue for taxes, log the sale date, not the payout date. Otherwise, your December sales that get paid in January will mess up your accounting. Once you establish yourself, the rhythm becomes predictable, and you can focus on sourcing instead of refreshing your app.
