When Do You Get Paid on Depop? Payout Time, Pending Money & Withdrawals (2026)

If you're waiting for money from a Depop sale, the confusing part isn't always the payout rule itself. It can be figuring out where your money is right now, why it is still pending, and when it should actually reach your bank.

Depop's current payout process depends on your country, shipping method, delivery status, and whether your payment has already been released to your Depop Balance.

Here's how it works.

LAST REVIEWED: SEPTEMBER 2026

Quick answer: when does Depop pay you?

Seller locationWhen Depop sends the payout
USGenerally 2 business days after delivery, or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first
UK — Depop ShippingGenerally 2 business days after delivery, or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first
UK — your own shippingGenerally 10 business days after delivery
Australia — Depop Shipping / tracked AusPostGenerally 2–3 business days after delivery, or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first
Australia — other shippingGenerally 10 business days after the sale

After Depop sends the money, your bank may take additional time to show it.

For US sellers, Depop says the payout is sent to the Depop Balance 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. After you withdraw available funds, bank processing can take additional time. UK and Australia guidance also notes that bank processing can take additional time.

So there are really two separate clocks:

Depop payout→Bank arrival

If your money has already reached your available Depop Balance, you're no longer waiting for Depop to release the sale proceeds. You're waiting for the withdrawal to reach your bank.

Where is your Depop money right now?

Before worrying about a late payout, check which state your money is actually in.

StatusWhat it generally means
PendingThe money isn't available to withdraw yet. Delivery, payout timing, or another requirement may still be outstanding.
AvailableThe funds are available in your Depop Balance and can be withdrawn, subject to your account setup.
On holdDepop may be holding the funds because of a buyer issue, dispute, or another account/payment review.
Negative balanceYour account currently owes money, which can affect future payouts and withdrawals.

This distinction matters because "my sale hasn't paid me" can mean several completely different things.

Depop payout time has two clocks

A lot of payout confusion comes from treating the whole process as one transaction.

It isn't.

Clock 1: When Depop releases the money

Depop looks at things such as:

  • whether the order has been delivered
  • when the sale happened
  • your shipping method
  • your country
  • whether there is an issue with the transaction

For many sellers, delivery is the event that starts the shorter payout window.

Clock 2: When your bank shows the money

Once the payout has been sent or withdrawn, your bank has its own processing time.

That means:

Delivered does not necessarily mean money in your bank today.

And:

Available in Depop Balance does not necessarily mean available in your bank account yet.

Try this before contacting Depop

If you're checking your payout because the money hasn't arrived, start with these four questions:

  1. Does Depop show the order as delivered?
    If not, the payout may still be waiting for the delivery event.
  2. Is the money Pending or Available?
    If it's Pending, the issue is probably before the withdrawal stage.
    If it's Available, the issue may be the withdrawal or bank stage.
  3. Did you actually withdraw the available balance?
    Depop's newer balance system has caused some sellers to expect an automatic bank deposit when the money is actually sitting in their Depop Balance waiting to be withdrawn.
  4. Are your bank details and verification complete?
    A payout can fail or remain unavailable if your bank information, identity verification, or other account requirements aren't complete.

What does "Pending" mean on Depop?

Pending generally means the funds aren't available to withdraw yet.

One common reason is that Depop hasn't registered the order as delivered.

This can be especially confusing when tracking shows that the package has physically arrived but the Depop order hasn't updated yet.

Other possibilities include:

  • the applicable payout window hasn't passed
  • the shipping method affects the payout schedule
  • a buyer issue or transaction review is affecting the payment
  • your account needs verification
  • your bank information needs attention

Don't assume that Pending = lost money. First find out which part of the process is still incomplete.

What does "Available" mean?

An Available balance is different.

If the money is showing as available in your Depop Balance, the sale proceeds have moved past the initial pending stage.

The next question is:

Have you withdrawn it to your bank?

This distinction became particularly noticeable after Depop introduced newer balance and withdrawal flows for some sellers.

What sellers are reporting

This isn't just a theoretical distinction.

In public seller discussions, multiple Depop sellers have described discovering that money had accumulated in their Depop Balance and that they needed to manually withdraw it rather than simply waiting for the bank deposit they expected.

One March 2026 discussion described confusion around the newer payout flow, while another seller in April reported asking how long a withdrawal should take after the money was already available. Similar discussions continued into May and June.

These are seller experiences rather than Depop's official payout rules, but the recurring pattern is useful: if your balance says Available, check the withdrawal step before assuming Depop hasn't paid you.

My Depop order says Delivered. Why haven't I been paid?

This is one of the most important situations to diagnose correctly.

If the package is delivered but your money hasn't arrived, check these in order:

1. Check the Depop delivery status

A carrier's tracking page and Depop's own order status don't always update at exactly the same time.

A seller reported an April 2026 case where an item had been delivered but Depop had not registered the delivery correctly, requiring support to manually address the payout.

That doesn't mean every delayed payout is a tracking problem. It means the delivery status inside Depop is worth checking before assuming the payout clock has started.

2. Check whether the money is Pending or Available

This tells you which part of the process you're dealing with.

Pending: investigate delivery, payout timing, verification, or an account issue.
Available: investigate the withdrawal and bank side.

3. Check your payout date

If Depop displays an expected payout date, compare it with today's date rather than judging the delay from the delivery date alone.

4. Check your bank information

Make sure:

  • the linked bank account is correct
  • the account can receive the payout
  • required verification is complete
  • the last four digits match the account you expect to receive the money

5. Check for a negative balance

A negative Depop Balance can affect what you actually receive.

Why does my Depop payout date keep changing?

This is another situation sellers can find surprisingly stressful.

You may see an expected payout date and then notice it move.

Before assuming Depop has taken your money, check:

  • whether the order is actually marked Delivered
  • whether the payout is Pending or Available
  • whether there is a buyer issue
  • whether your bank details or verification need attention
  • whether the displayed date is an estimate rather than the moment the money will appear in your bank

Sellers have also reported seeing an available balance while the bank transfer took additional time to arrive.

The important thing is to separate Depop's release of the money from your bank's receipt of the money.

How long does a Depop withdrawal take?

Once you've withdrawn available funds, the remaining wait is largely on the banking side.

For US sellers, Depop currently says that after you withdraw available funds, bank processing can take additional time.

Seller discussions show why this can feel longer in practice.

Some sellers report waiting several days after withdrawing, while others say the money arrived sooner. Weekend timing can also make a short calendar delay feel longer.

So if you withdrew on Friday and are checking repeatedly over the weekend, don't automatically treat that as a failed payout.

What if my Depop payout fails?

A failed payout is different from a payout that is simply taking time.

Check:

  • bank account details
  • whether your bank account is still valid
  • identity verification
  • whether Depop is asking for additional information
  • whether your account has a negative balance

If the payout continues to fail after you've checked those items, contact Depop support with the transaction details.

What if Depop is asking me to verify my identity?

Verification can be part of the payment process.

If Depop requires verification, complete it using the information requested in your account.

A payout problem isn't always caused by the sale itself. Sometimes the transaction is fine and the payment can't proceed because the account isn't fully set up for payouts.

What does a negative Depop balance mean?

A negative balance deserves more attention than an ordinary delayed payout.

It means your account currently has a negative amount rather than money available to withdraw.

This can happen after things such as refunds or other account adjustments.

There have also been recurring seller reports of unexpected negative balances, including sellers who said they had not knowingly issued a refund or dispute.

Those reports are worth knowing about, but the cause shouldn't be guessed from Reddit speculation.

If you see an unexpected negative balance:

  • Check your transaction history.
  • Look for refunds or adjustments.
  • Check whether a previous transaction changed.
  • Review your Depop Balance.
  • Contact Depop if you cannot identify the transaction creating the negative amount.

Depop's current terms also explain that a negative balance can represent an amount owed to Depop and may affect account access or future payment collection.

A refund can affect your balance later

This is important if you're tracking your money sale by sale.

A sale doesn't necessarily remain financially unchanged forever.

A later refund or transaction adjustment can change your balance after the original payout.

Depop's current refund guidance says that, for Depop Payments, the relevant payment processing fee is automatically refunded when a refund is processed.

So if you're trying to reconcile a negative balance, don't look only at today's sales.

Look backward through the affected transaction history.

What if the buyer says the item arrived, but Depop hasn't marked it delivered?

This is where tracking becomes important.

If the carrier says Delivered but Depop still shows the order as undelivered:

  • keep the tracking information
  • check the tracking number against the transaction
  • make sure the carrier status is genuinely Delivered
  • give the platform's tracking system time to update
  • contact support if the payout remains stuck beyond the applicable window

Keep your shipping receipt and tracking information.

They can become useful evidence if you need support to investigate the transaction.

What sellers are actually experiencing

Official Depop documentation tells us how the payout system is supposed to work.

Public seller discussions show something different: where people are getting confused in real life.

Seller reports throughout 2026 repeatedly mention:

  • money sitting in Depop Balance
  • confusion about manual withdrawals
  • bank arrival taking longer than expected
  • delivered orders that don't immediately trigger the expected payout
  • payout dates changing
  • occasional unexpected negative balances

For example, sellers discussing withdrawals in April, July, and September have described waiting several days after initiating a withdrawal. Other sellers reported that their money became available in Depop but had not yet appeared in their bank.

These reports don't replace Depop's official rules. They're useful because they show the questions sellers are actually running into after those rules meet real-world transactions.

That's why the most useful way to troubleshoot a payout is not simply asking:

"How long does Depop take?"

Instead ask:

"Which stage is my money currently stuck at?"

Depop payout timing by country

United States

For US sellers using Depop Payments, Depop currently states that payouts are generally sent 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.

The money then needs to reach your bank.

If the funds are already available in your Depop Balance, check whether you've withdrawn them.

United Kingdom

For sellers using Depop Shipping through Evri, Depop currently describes a payout schedule of 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.

If you use your own shipping method, the applicable timing can instead be 10 business days after delivery.

Bank processing happens separately after the payout is sent.

Australia

For Depop Shipping or tracked AusPost shipments, Depop currently describes payouts as generally occurring 2–3 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.

For other shipping methods, the payout timing can instead be 10 business days after the sale.

Your bank may then require additional processing time.

Do weekends and holidays matter?

They can.

The payout rules use business-day calculations in several places, so a weekend or holiday can make the calendar gap look longer than the actual processing period.

The bank stage is also separate.

So don't compare:

"It was delivered Friday."

with:

"It's Monday and I don't have the money."

and immediately conclude that something has failed.

Look at the actual payout status and applicable schedule first.

What can delay a Depop payout?

The most useful checklist is:

  • Delivery hasn't been registered — The carrier may show delivery while Depop still hasn't updated the order.
  • Your payout window hasn't finished — The sale may simply still be inside the applicable payout schedule.
  • The money is Available but hasn't been withdrawn — This is particularly important with newer balance workflows.
  • Bank details need attention — Incorrect or incomplete information can prevent a successful payout.
  • Identity verification isn't complete — Depop may require verification before funds can be withdrawn.
  • A transaction is on hold — A buyer issue, dispute, or review can affect the payment.
  • Your balance is negative — A negative balance can change what happens to subsequent funds.

What should I send Depop support?

If you've gone through the checklist and the payout is still stuck, don't send a vague message like:

"Where's my money?"

Give support enough information to investigate the specific transaction.

Include:

  • your Depop username
  • transaction/order information
  • sale date
  • delivery date
  • payout date shown in the app, if available
  • payout amount
  • current balance status
  • whether you've already withdrawn the funds
  • relevant tracking information
  • screenshots showing the issue

This makes it much easier to explain where the payout process appears to have stopped.

A simple way to diagnose your payout

Think of your sale as four stages:

SALE COMPLETED↓DELIVERY REGISTERED↓FUNDS RELEASED / AVAILABLE↓WITHDRAWAL → BANK

Now find the first stage that hasn't happened.

Stuck before delivery? Check tracking and delivery status.
Delivered but still Pending? Check the applicable payout window and account status.
Available but not in your bank? Check whether you've withdrawn it and then consider bank processing time.
Negative balance? Review refunds and account adjustments.
Everything looks correct but the payout still hasn't arrived? Contact Depop with the transaction evidence.

This is usually much more useful than repeatedly refreshing the balance.

Frequently asked questions

When do you get paid on Depop?

It depends on your country and shipping method. In the US, Depop generally sends payment 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. Other countries and shipping methods can have different schedules.

Do Depop sellers get paid immediately after a sale?

Not necessarily. The payout can depend on delivery, the applicable payout schedule, account verification, and your shipping method.

Does Depop pay you after the item is delivered?

For many shipping setups, delivery is an important trigger for the payout schedule. However, the exact timing depends on your country and shipping method.

Why is my Depop money still pending?

Possible reasons include the order not being registered as delivered, the payout window not being complete, a transaction issue, or an account/verification requirement.

My Depop money says Available. Why isn't it in my bank?

If the money is Available in your Depop Balance, check whether you've withdrawn it. After withdrawal, the bank may need additional processing time.

How long does a Depop withdrawal take?

After withdrawal, your bank may take additional processing time. Actual arrival can vary by bank and timing.

Why did my Depop payout fail?

Check your linked bank details, verification status, and account balance first. If everything looks correct and the payout continues to fail, contact Depop support.

Why does my Depop balance say negative?

A negative balance means your account currently owes money rather than having funds available to withdraw. Check your transaction history for refunds or other adjustments.

Can a refund affect my Depop Balance?

Yes. A later refund or transaction adjustment can change your balance. For Depop Payments, Depop says the applicable payment processing fee is automatically refunded when a refund is processed.

Do Depop reviews affect when I get paid?

Depop's current payout guidance focuses on delivery, sale timing, shipping method, and account/payment requirements rather than buyer reviews as a general payout trigger.

The takeaway

If you're waiting for a Depop payout, don't treat "not in my bank" as one single problem.

First find out where the money is:

Pending→Available→Withdrawn→Bank

That tells you what to check next.

For most sellers, the biggest source of confusion is that Depop's payout process and your bank's processing time are two different stages. Add delivery tracking, verification, shipping method, refunds, and the newer balance workflow, and a simple question like "When does Depop pay me?" can become surprisingly complicated.

Once you know which stage you're actually waiting on, the problem becomes much easier to diagnose.
