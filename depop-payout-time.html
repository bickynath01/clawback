When Do I Get Paid on Depop? Exact Payout Times (2026)? Payout Times, Holds, and Delays Explained

You did everything right. You photographed the jacket, priced it perfectly (after figuring out exactly what Depop takes), packaged it securely, and dropped it in the mail. The tracking just updated to "Delivered." You refresh your Depop app, expecting to see that cash sitting in your balance, ready to be withdrawn. But it’s not there. Or worse—it's there, but the "Withdraw" button is greyed out.

If you've ever sat there refreshing your screen asking "when does Depop actually pay you?", you are not alone. Getting paid on Depop isn't instant, and the rules around Depop payout times, new seller holds, and delayed funds are some of the most confusing parts of the platform. This is the plain-English guide to exactly when the money hits your bank, why it might be stuck, and how to get it moving faster.

The short answer: For established sellers, Depop releases funds 2 to 10 working days after the tracking shows "Delivered." For brand-new sellers, funds can be held for up to 21 to 30 days while Depop verifies your account. Bank transfers then take an additional 1 to 3 business days to clear.

The Anatomy of a Depop Payout: Day by Day

To understand when you get paid, you have to understand that Depop doesn't release the money the second the buyer clicks "Buy." They hold it in escrow to protect the buyer. Here is the exact timeline of a standard Depop payout:

THE STANDARD PAYOUT TIMELINE

DAY 1
The Sale. The buyer pays. Depop takes their money and holds it securely. You see a "Sold" notification, but the funds are pending.
DAY 2-4
The Shipping Window. You pack and ship the item. Once you buy your shipping label or upload the tracking number, Depop knows the item is actually moving.
DAY 5-7
Delivery. The carrier scans the package as "Delivered." This is the trigger event. The clock for your payout officially starts here.
DAY 7-10
The Release. Depop waits a few days after delivery to ensure the buyer doesn't open a "Not As Described" dispute. If all is quiet, the funds are released to your available balance.
DAY 11+
Bank Transfer. You click withdraw. The money moves from Depop to your bank account, taking 1 to 3 business days depending on your bank.

Why is my Depop payout delayed? (The 4 Red Flags)

If it’s been over 10 days since delivery and your Depop funds are still pending or on hold, one of these four things is happening. Don't panic—most of these are automatic security measures.

1. You forgot to add the tracking number

This is the #1 reason for delayed payouts. If you bought a label outside of Depop (like at the post office) and just mailed it without typing the tracking number into the app, Depop has no idea the item was delivered. Without a "Delivered" scan from an integrated carrier, Depop will hold the funds indefinitely or until the buyer manually confirms receipt. Always, always upload the tracking number.

2. The buyer opened a dispute or requested a refund

If the buyer claims the item never arrived, or they open a case saying it’s not as described, Depop immediately freezes the payout. The funds will not be released until the dispute is resolved. This is why photographing your items before shipping is non-negotiable.

3. You are a brand-new seller (The 21-Day Hold)

When you first open a Depop shop, you are a massive fraud risk in their eyes. Scammers frequently create new accounts, sell fake designer goods, take the money, and vanish. To combat this, Depop places a new seller rolling reserve on your account. They may hold your funds for 21 to 30 days to ensure you are a legitimate seller shipping real items. As you build up 5-star reviews, this hold shrinks and eventually disappears.

4. You had a sudden spike in sales

If you normally sell two items a week, and suddenly you sell $2,000 worth of vintage denim in a single weekend, Depop’s automated security bots will flag your account. A sudden spike looks like a hacked account or a scammer cashing out. They will temporarily pause your payouts while they manually review your account. If this happens, just verify your identity in the app settings and the hold will lift.

Does Depop pay on weekends or holidays?

No. Depop payouts are processed through standard banking networks (like ACH in the US or BACS in the UK). These networks do not operate on weekends or bank holidays. If your funds are released on a Friday afternoon, the actual bank transfer might not initiate until Monday morning, meaning you won't see the money until Tuesday or Wednesday. Always calculate your cash flow around business days, not calendar days.

How to get paid faster on Depop (Pro Tips)

You can't change Depop's core rules, but you can avoid the delays that sellers accidentally trigger. Do these three things on every single sale:

  1. Ship within 24 hours. The faster it gets marked "Delivered," the faster the 2-to-10-day clock starts.
  2. Use Depop's integrated shipping or upload tracking immediately. Never mail an item without the tracking number being inside the app. No tracking = no payout.
  3. Message the buyer on delivery day. Send a quick, polite message: "Hi! Tracking says delivered. Hope you love it! If everything is great, I'd so appreciate a 5-star rating."s When a buyer leaves a 5-star rating, it signals to Depop that the transaction is complete, which can speed up the release of funds.

The Tax Reality of Depop Payouts

Just because Depop holds your money for 10 days doesn't mean it's not "yours" for tax purposes. In the eyes of the IRS (and most tax authorities), you earned the income the moment the sale was made, not the moment the money hit your bank account. When you are tracking your yearly revenue for taxes, log the sale date, not the payout date. Otherwise, your December sales that get paid in January will mess up your accounting. Conclusion: Stop refreshing, start tracking

The anxiety of waiting for a Depop payout is a rite of passage for every seller. The money is there, it’s just moving through the banking and security gears. Understand the 2-to-10-day window, always upload your tracking, and accept that as a new seller, the first few payouts will take a month. Once you establish yourself, the rhythm becomes predictable, and you can focus on sourcing instead of refreshing your app.
