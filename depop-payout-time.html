Does Depop Pay on Weekends? Why Your Payout Date Keeps Changing

Quick answer: No — Depop does not guarantee Saturday or Sunday payouts. Its published schedules count business days, so a weekend or bank holiday can shift when funds become available or reach your bank. A moved expected date is a clue to investigate, not proof of a delay: start with the payout stage shown in your Depop account.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

You sell an item, ship it, and watch the tracking update. Then you check your Depop account and notice that your expected payout date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or around a bank holiday.

Naturally, you want to know whether the money will arrive that day or whether you need to wait longer.

The important distinction: a payout schedule measured in business days is not the same as a guaranteed bank-arrival date. Depop's published guidance uses business-day timelines for several payout arrangements, but the official information reviewed for this guide does not establish a universal rule that every weekend delays every Depop payment. Your expected date, the payout arrangement that applies to your sale, and the payment information in your account are more useful than the day of the week alone.

Here's how to think about weekend payouts, changing dates, and what to check before contacting Depop.

Does Depop pay on weekends?

You should not rely on a Depop payout arriving on Saturday or Sunday unless the payment information available for your transaction supports that expectation.

Depop's published payout schedules use business days for several arrangements. When a timeline is expressed in business days, Saturday and Sunday should not automatically be counted as business days.

However, this does not prove that every payout stops over the weekend. The timing of a particular payment depends on the applicable payout schedule and the stage your transaction has reached. For example, if your item is marked delivered on Friday, that alone does not tell you whether the funds will become available over the weekend or when a subsequent bank transfer will appear.

Keep these two questions separate:

Payout timing: When does Depop make the sale proceeds available? vs Bank-arrival timing: When will your bank display it?

The day of the week is only one part of the picture. Check the expected payout information in your Depop account before deciding that your payment is late.

Does Depop pay on Saturdays?

Do not assume that your payout will arrive on Saturday simply because the expected date shown in your account falls on that day.

If your applicable payout schedule uses business days, Saturday may not count toward the stated processing period. But the official guidance reviewed for this article does not establish that all Depop payments are prevented from arriving on Saturdays.

If you are waiting on a Saturday:

  1. Check the expected payout information shown in your Depop account.
  2. Confirm whether the date refers to funds becoming available or a bank transfer arriving.
  3. Look for a payment notice, error, or account requirement.
  4. If the applicable window has passed, follow Depop's current support guidance.

Avoid treating Saturday morning as a universal deadline. The relevant schedule and transaction information are more useful than an assumed arrival time.

Does Depop pay on Sundays?

A Sunday payout is not something you should rely on without payment-specific information.

Where a payout or transfer timeline uses business days, Sunday should not automatically be counted as a business day. That affects how you estimate the remaining time, but it does not prove that a particular payment has been delayed because of Sunday.

If your expected date falls on a Sunday, check whether Depop has sent or released the payment and whether your bank is still processing it.

If you cannot tell which stage your payment has reached, read our broader guide: When Do You Get Paid on Depop? Payout Time, Pending Money & Withdrawals. That guide covers the general payout timeline, balance statuses, withdrawals, and troubleshooting. This article stays focused on weekends and expected-date changes.

Can a bank holiday delay a Depop payout?

A bank holiday can affect your estimate when the applicable payout schedule or bank transfer relies on business days.

But a holiday on the calendar does not automatically prove that your specific Depop payment has been delayed for that reason. The effect can depend on your country, payment arrangement, and the stage of the transaction.

If a bank holiday falls close to your expected payout date:

  • Check the payout information in your Depop account.
  • Use the business-day rules that apply to your country and payment arrangement.
  • Distinguish between Depop making the funds available and your bank displaying a transfer.
  • Check for account or transaction notices before attributing the delay to the holiday.

Do not assume that the money must arrive on the next calendar day after a holiday. Use the relevant payment information rather than guessing.

Why does my Depop payout date keep changing?

Seeing an expected payout date move can be frustrating, especially when you are relying on that money. But a changing date is a reason to investigate, not a diagnosis by itself. Several different things may affect your expectations.

Business-day timing

If the applicable payout schedule counts business days, a weekend or holiday can affect how you estimate the remaining time.

Bank processing

Depop sending a payment and your bank displaying the funds are separate events. A date associated with one stage does not necessarily promise an arrival time for the other.

Delivery or account information

Delivery information, an account requirement, or a transaction issue may also matter. These are not the same as ordinary business-day timing.

Do not assume that the weekend is the cause simply because your expected date changed. Check the information available in your account to establish which stage your payment has reached and whether Depop is asking you to take action. If you need help identifying the stage where your payment has stopped, use the main Depop payout troubleshooting guide.

What time does Depop pay on Saturday or Sunday?

There is no universal Saturday or Sunday arrival time you should rely on for every Depop payout.

If the money has not appeared by Saturday morning, repeatedly checking the app throughout the day will not tell you whether the payment is following its schedule or whether an account issue needs attention.

Instead, check the expected date and payment information shown in your account. If a bank transfer has already been initiated, distinguish its processing time from the time it took for the sale proceeds to become available. Do not rely on an exact clock-time prediction unless Depop or your bank provides one for your specific transaction.

When should you stop waiting and check your payout?

A weekend on the calendar is not, by itself, a reason to panic. But you should investigate if the applicable payout window has passed or your account shows a problem.

Start with these three checks:

  1. Check the expected payout information. Compare it with the schedule that applies to your sale rather than counting every calendar day as a business day.
  2. Read the payment status and notifications. Look for a status, error, or account requirement that needs your attention.
  3. Follow Depop's instructions if the expected window has passed. If the payment information indicates a problem you cannot resolve, contact Depop through its current official support guidance.

Do not assume that every late payment is caused by weekends. Likewise, do not assume your money is lost simply because it has not appeared in your bank yet. For a step-by-step explanation of Pending and Available balances, withdrawals, and other payout problems, see our Depop payout time and troubleshooting guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does Depop pay on Saturdays?

Do not assume a payout will arrive on Saturday. Where the applicable timeline uses business days, Saturday should not automatically be counted as a business day. Check the expected date and payment information in your Depop account.

Does Depop pay on Sundays?

You should not rely on a Sunday arrival without payment-specific information. Business-day timelines can affect your estimate, but Sunday alone does not prove that your payment is delayed.

Can a bank holiday affect my Depop payout?

It can affect your estimate when the relevant payout or bank-processing timeline uses business days. The holiday alone does not establish why a particular payment has or has not arrived, so check your account and the policy that applies to your transaction.

Why did my expected Depop payout date change?

The date alone does not identify the cause. Business-day timing, bank processing, delivery information, or an account or transaction requirement may be relevant. Check your payment details before assuming the weekend is responsible.

Should I contact Depop if my payout is late?

First compare your payment with the applicable payout window and check for status or account messages. If the relevant window has passed or your account shows a payment problem, follow Depop's current support guidance.
