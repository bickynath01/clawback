When Do You Get Paid on Depop? Payout Time, Pending Money & Withdrawals

LAST UPDATED:12 OCTOBER 2026

By CLAWBACK Editorial Team

Quick answer: For US Depop Payments, sale proceeds reach your Depop Balance 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale — whichever comes first — and a bank transfer then takes up to 5 days to appear. If money looks late, check whether your balance is Pending, Available, On hold or negative before assuming the payment failed.

You have made a sale on Depop, shipped the item, and perhaps even watched the tracking update to Delivered. So where is your money?

The confusing part is that the time Depop makes your sale proceeds available and the time the money reaches your bank account are two different things.

For US sellers using Depop Payments, Depop's published guidance says it sends payment to your Depop Balance 2 business days after the item shows as delivered or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. After you withdraw available funds, the bank transfer can take up to 5 days to appear.

Your exact payout schedule may differ depending on your country, shipping method, and payment arrangement. Account verification, a transaction hold, or a problem with your bank details can also affect your payment. This guide explains when Depop pays sellers, what Pending and Available mean, and what to do if your money has not arrived yet.

Quick answer: When does Depop pay you?

For US sellers using Depop Payments, Depop's published guidance says payment is sent to your Depop Balance 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. Once the funds are available, you can withdraw them to your connected bank account. Depop says a US bank transfer can take up to 5 days to appear after withdrawal.

Payout stageWhat it means

The important distinction: an order marked Delivered does not automatically mean the money is already in your bank. An Available balance also does not necessarily mean a bank transfer has been initiated. Start by identifying the stage your payment has reached. That will help you decide what to check next.

How long does Depop take to pay you?

Depop's payout timing varies by country and shipping arrangement. The US, UK, and Australian schedules should not be treated as interchangeable.

US sellers

According to Depop's official US payout guidance:

  • Depop sends payment to your Depop Balance 2 business days after the item shows as delivered or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.
  • Pending funds are not available to withdraw.
  • Once funds become available, you can withdraw them if your account supports withdrawals and the required setup is complete.
  • A bank transfer can take up to 5 days to appear after withdrawal.
  • You need a valid connected bank account and may be asked to complete additional identity verification.

If your account shows a different payout arrangement, follow the current instructions displayed in Depop.

UK sellers

In the UK, payout timing depends on how the order was shipped. Depop's published UK guidance says:

  • Depop Shipping: payment is sent 2 business days after the item shows as delivered or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first.
  • Manual shipping: payment is sent 10 business days after the order has been delivered.

Depop says funds can take up to 5 days to appear in your bank account after they are sent. Make sure your bank details and identity verification are complete if required.

Australian sellers

Australian sellers should check Depop's current payout guidance for their shipping method and payment arrangement. The payout trigger and expected timing may differ depending on how the order was shipped. Do not automatically apply the US or UK schedule to an Australian account.

Remember: a published payout timeline is not a guarantee of an exact deposit hour. Your order status, account requirements, payment arrangement, and bank processing all matter.

What do Pending and Available mean on Depop?

If you are wondering why Depop has not paid you yet, check your balance status first.

What does Pending mean on Depop?

A Pending balance means your funds are not yet available for withdrawal. The funds may still be waiting for the applicable delivery or payout milestone. Check these things:

  • Does the order show as Delivered inside Depop?
  • Has the relevant payout period passed?
  • Is there a payment notification or identity-verification request?
  • Is there a transaction issue affecting the funds?

Do not assume Pending means your money is lost. First check the order details, payment activity, and instructions in your account.

What does Available mean on Depop?

An Available balance means your funds have reached the available stage in your Depop Balance. If the money has not reached your bank, check whether your account requires you to initiate a withdrawal or whether the payout is already scheduled. If a transfer has already started, look at its status and date. Then check your connected bank account and the relevant bank-processing window.

What does On hold mean on Depop?

Depop explains that funds can be placed On hold when a buyer raises an issue with an order. This is different from an ordinary payout delay. Check the notification in your account and follow Depop's instructions for resolving the issue.

What does a negative Depop balance mean?

A negative balance can occur when a refund is issued but there is not enough money in your balance to cover it. Depop explains that a negative balance can affect future payments because new payments may be used to offset the negative amount. If your balance is lower than you expected, review your refund history and balance activity. If you need to understand how fees and refunds affect a sale, read our guide to Depop fees and refunds.

My Depop order says Delivered. Why haven't I been paid?

If your package has arrived but your money has not, work through these checks in order.

1. Check the order status inside Depop

Start with the order page in Depop, not only the carrier's tracking website. If the carrier shows a delivery scan but Depop does not show the order as delivered, the information used for the payout process may not yet match. Keep your tracking details and follow Depop's current instructions if the two statuses appear inconsistent.

2. Check your balance status

Your balance status helps identify the next step.

  • Pending: Check the delivery status, applicable payout schedule, and account notifications.
  • Available: Check whether a withdrawal or payout has been initiated.
  • On hold: Review the buyer issue or instructions from Depop.
  • Negative balance: Review refunds and balance activity.

3. Compare the dates with the correct payout schedule

If Depop specifies business days, do not count every calendar day as a business day. For example, the standard US Depop Payments schedule uses 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. Check the rules that apply to your account before deciding that your payment is overdue.

4. Check your bank details and verification status

Depop may require a valid connected bank account and identity verification before you can withdraw money. Check if the correct bank account is connected, if your details match the information requested by Depop, and if there is an error or notification in your payment settings.

5. Contact Depop if the issue remains unresolved

If the applicable payout period has passed and your account details appear correct, follow Depop's official support guidance. When contacting support, include the order details, tracking information, your balance status, the expected payout information, and any error message.

How long does a Depop bank withdrawal take?

For US sellers, Depop says a bank transfer can take up to 5 days to appear after withdrawal. That period is separate from the time it takes for sale proceeds to become available in your Depop Balance.

For example, your funds might already be Available while the bank transfer is still processing. In that situation, repeatedly checking the delivery status will not tell you much. Check the withdrawal or payout status instead. If the applicable bank-arrival window has passed, or the transfer is marked as failed, review the transaction details and follow Depop's support instructions.

Why won't Depop let me withdraw my money?

If Depop will not let you withdraw your funds, first confirm that your balance is Available and check whether your account supports manual withdrawals. Then review these possible causes:

  • Your balance is still Pending: Check the delivery status, applicable payout timeline, and account notifications.
  • Your bank account is missing or incorrect: Review your payment settings and correct any details that are wrong.
  • Your identity verification is incomplete: Follow the request shown in your account.
  • Your balance is negative: Review your refund and balance history.
  • Your withdrawal has failed: Open the payment or withdrawal details and read the error message. Incorrect bank details or a restricted account may prevent a transfer.

Does Depop pay on weekends?

Depop uses business-day timelines for several payout arrangements. When a payout period is expressed in business days, Saturday and Sunday should not automatically be counted as business days. However, that does not establish a universal rule that every payout stops over a weekend or that every payment delayed over a weekend will arrive on Monday.

There are two separate questions:

  1. When does Depop release your sale proceeds under the schedule that applies to your account?
  2. After the money is sent, when will your bank display it?

A weekend or bank holiday can affect your estimate of a business-day window or bank processing. It does not, by itself, prove that your payout is overdue or that something is wrong with your account. If your main question is about Saturdays, Sundays, bank holidays, or an expected payout date that keeps changing, read our focused guide: Does Depop Pay on Weekends? What Sellers Should Know.

Can a refund affect my Depop Balance or the money I keep?

Yes. A refund can affect the amount remaining in your balance. If a refund is issued when your balance is too low to cover it, you may end up with a negative balance. But there are two different questions to consider:

  • Why has my payment not arrived? Check your balance status, payout schedule, account setup, and transfer details.
  • Has this refund or return cost me money? Check the refund, fee treatment, shipping expenses, and condition of the returned item.

Depop's official seller-fees policy says that when a sale is refunded, selling fees, payment-processing fees, and boosting fees are reversed. For Depop Payments, this happens automatically. Sellers outside the UK, US, and Australia who use PayPal must contact Depop Support to request a refund of the Depop selling fee. Even if applicable fees are reversed, you may still want to check other costs associated with the return, such as return shipping or a drop in resale value.

Frequently asked questions

When do you get paid on Depop?

For US sellers using Depop Payments, Depop says it sends payment to Depop Balance 2 business days after delivery or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. Other countries and shipping arrangements can follow different schedules.

Why is my Depop balance still Pending after delivery?

Check whether Depop itself shows the order as delivered, then compare the dates with the payout schedule for your country and shipping method. Also check for account or transaction notifications.

What should I do if my Depop balance is Available but the money is not in my bank?

Check whether a withdrawal or payout has been initiated. If it has, review the transfer date, status, connected bank details, and relevant bank-processing window.

How long does a Depop bank withdrawal take?

Depop says a US bank transfer can take up to 5 days to appear after withdrawal. This is separate from the time it takes for sale proceeds to become available in Depop Balance.

Why won't Depop let me withdraw money?

First confirm that your balance is Available and check whether your account supports manual withdrawals. Then review your bank details, identity-verification status, balance activity, and any withdrawal error or account notice.

Can a negative Depop balance affect my payout?

Yes. A negative balance can reduce the amount available to you because new payments may be used to offset the negative amount. Review your refund and balance activity to understand what caused it.

Does Depop pay on Saturdays, Sundays, or bank holidays?

Depop uses business-day timelines for several payout arrangements, but the timing depends on your country and shipping method. A weekend alone does not prove that your payment is overdue. Read our Depop weekend payout guide for more detail.

Can a Depop refund leave me with a loss?

It can, but not every refund creates a loss. Check the fee treatment, any shipping expenses you paid, the item's condition after return, and any value you recovered. If you need help examining the financial impact, use the CLAWBACK return-loss calculator.

When should I contact Depop about a delayed payout?

Check the applicable payout window, delivery status, balance status, bank details, verification requirements, and any payment error first. If the relevant window has passed or your account shows a problem you cannot resolve, follow Depop's official support instructions.

The takeaway

When you are waiting for a Depop payout, first identify where the money is in the process. If it is Pending, check delivery status, the applicable payout schedule, and account notices. If it is Available, check whether a withdrawal or payout has been initiated. If a transfer has been sent, check the bank-arrival window and transaction status.

You do not need to guess at the reason for every delay. Start with the balance status, check the rules that apply to your account, and take the next step based on what you find.
