Does Depop Pay on Weekends? Why Your Payout Date Keeps Changing

You sell an item, pack it, ship it, and finally see the delivery update. Naturally, you start thinking about when that money will reach you.

Then Friday passes. Your payout still hasn't arrived, and you're left wondering: Does Depop pay on weekends, or am I going to have to wait until Monday?

If you rely on resale income to cover shipping supplies, buy more inventory, or simply keep your selling activity moving, waiting for a payout can be frustrating. The tricky part is knowing whether the weekend is relevant to your payment—or whether there's something else you need to check.

Let's focus on the weekend question, what it can and cannot tell you, and when it's worth looking more closely at your payment.

Does Depop Pay on Weekends?

The answer depends on your payout arrangement, country, and the stage your payment has reached. Depop's published payout rules use business-day timelines in some markets and shipping arrangements, so you shouldn't automatically treat Saturday and Sunday like ordinary processing days.

For example, if your item is marked delivered on Friday, don't assume the money must arrive over the weekend—or that the weekend itself explains any delay. The payout schedule that applies to your sale and the time your bank takes to display a transfer are separate considerations.

To avoid confusion, it helps to separate four different events:

  • The applicable Depop payout schedule (which may count business days).
  • Depop actually sending the payment to your bank.
  • Your bank processing the transfer and displaying the funds.
  • An account or transaction issue that may require your attention.

However, a weekend does not automatically mean your payout is delayed, and it does not prove that something is wrong with your account. The applicable schedule and the information shown in your Depop account are more useful than the day of the week alone.

If you're trying to work out the full timeline for your sale, our Depop payout time guide explains the broader process and what the different stages mean. This article stays focused on weekends; that guide is the better place to start when you need to understand your particular payment.

Why Does My Depop Payout Date Keep Changing?

Seeing an expected date move is frustrating, especially when you've already planned around that money. But a changing date is a clue to investigate—not a diagnosis on its own.

A few different things can affect your expectations:

  • Business-day timing: If your applicable payout schedule counts business days, a weekend can affect how you estimate the remaining time.
  • Bank processing: Depop sending a payment and your bank showing the money are not necessarily the same event.
  • Order or account requirements: Delivery information, a payment issue, or an account requirement may affect a payment independently of the weekend.

These possibilities shouldn't be treated as interchangeable. A payment that is following its applicable schedule is different from one affected by an account issue, and neither can be confirmed simply by looking at the calendar. That way, you're not just waiting and hoping—the next step is based on what your account actually shows.

What Time Does Depop Pay on Saturday or Sunday?

It is tempting to look for a particular hour when a payout might arrive. If the money hasn't appeared by Saturday morning, you might check again at lunchtime and then again in the evening.

Unfortunately, you shouldn't rely on a guaranteed Saturday or Sunday payout time unless Depop provides one for your particular payment. A specific clock-time prediction is not a dependable way to decide whether your money is late.

Instead, pay attention to the expected date and payment information available in your account. Those details are more useful than repeatedly refreshing the app.

Can Bank Holidays Affect a Depop Payout?

A weekend or bank holiday can affect your expectations when the applicable payout schedule or bank transfer relies on business days. However, a holiday on the calendar does not automatically prove that a specific payment is delayed for that reason.

The effect depends on your market, payment arrangement, and the exact stage of the transaction. Counting calendar days alone can be misleading. If a holiday falls near your expected payout date, check the current schedule that applies to your account rather than assuming the payment will arrive on the next calendar day.

When Should You Stop Waiting and Check Your Payout?

Waiting a little longer and investigating a payment problem are two different decisions. The aim isn't to panic over every weekend delay; it's to recognise when the available information calls for a closer look.

Start with these three checks:

  1. Look at the expected payout date. Compare it with the date shown in your Depop account and the payout schedule that applies to your sale.
  2. Read the payment information carefully. Check whether Depop shows a status, notification, error, or action that needs your attention.
  3. Take action if the expected window has passed. Follow Depop's current guidance if your payment is overdue or the app indicates a problem.

Don't assume that every late payment is caused by weekends. Likewise, don't assume your money is lost just because it hasn't appeared in your bank yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Depop pay sellers on Saturdays?

You shouldn't assume that a payout will arrive on Saturday. Timing depends on the applicable payout schedule and payment processing. Check the expected date and transaction information in your Depop account.

Does Depop pay sellers on Sundays?

A Sunday arrival is not something you should rely on without payment-specific information. Where business-day processing applies, weekends can affect the timing you should expect.

Why did my expected Depop payout date change?

The date alone does not identify the cause. Business-day timing, bank processing, delivery information, or an account or transaction requirement may be relevant. Check your payment details before assuming that the weekend is responsible.

Should I contact Depop if my payout is late?

First compare your payment with the applicable payout window and check for any status or account messages. If the expected window has passed or the app shows a payment problem, follow Depop's current support guidance.
