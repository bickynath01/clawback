Poshmark vs Depop vs Mercari vs eBay: Which Platform Actually Pays You More? (The 2026 Matrix)

Three hours at the thrift store just paid off — a vintage Levi's jacket, beautiful condition, genuinely profitable. Then the wall hits: where does this actually get listed?

Ask any reseller group and a dozen loud opinions show up instantly. "eBay has the traffic!" "Poshmark buyers pay more!" "Mercari is easiest!" Almost nobody runs the actual math. The truth is there's no single "best" platform — there's only the best platform for that specific item, at that price, at that weight. That's not a vague answer, either — it's a calculation, and it's below.

The official 2026 fee schedules, shipping policies and return rules were pulled from all four platforms, then real items got run through every single one. Welcome to The Reseller's Matrix.

The TL;DR: Heavy items (shoes, electronics) win on Mercari or eBay. Trendy women's vintage wins on Poshmark despite the 20% fee. Y2K streetwear wins on Depop. But read the shipping traps below before listing a single item — the trap, not the base fee, is usually what actually kills profit.

The Bundle Economy: Who Actually Profits?

Bundles are where resellers quietly win or lose the month. On Poshmark, bundles are the whole culture — buyers expect to combine items for one $6.49 label. Great for moving volume, but remember the 5lb wall: a bundle tipping past 5 lbs triggers a $5–$10 fee deducted straight from earnings. Weigh before approving.

On eBay, combined shipping is a seller's choice — calculated shipping can automatically discount when a buyer adds items, protecting margin. On Mercari, bundles are manual: buyers ask, the price adjusts, one heavier package ships on a heavier prepaid label. On Depop, bundles are informal — agree on a price, ship it independently. The platform that rewards bundling most is Poshmark; the platform that punishes a heavy bundle hardest is also Poshmark. Know which side of the 5lb line a closet lives on.

Round 2: The Shipping Nightmares

Poshmark: The 5-Pound Bundle Trap

Poshmark's flat $6.49 buyer shipping and prepaid label are genuinely simple — until a buyer bundles. Two pairs of jeans pushing a package to 5.1 lbs voids the flat label, and Poshmark deducts a $5 overweight upgrade fee straight from earnings. At 10.1 lbs it's $10. Always weigh before approving a bundle.

Mercari: The 100lb Heavyweight Champion

Mercari's prepaid labels support packages up to 100 pounds — unbeatable for shoes, electronics and car parts. Weigh accurately either way: underpaid-label overage fees come straight out of the balance.

Depop & eBay: Calculated Chaos

Depop leaves shipping math entirely up to the seller. eBay's calculated shipping is accurate, but a missed handling-time deadline spikes the Late Shipment Rate and risks seller standing.

New-Seller Reality: Holds, Reserves & Verification

Brand-new accounts get treated as a fraud risk everywhere, but the severity differs. Depop is strictest: expect a 21–30 day rolling reserve while history builds. eBay may place payment holds on new or below-standard accounts, sometimes until tracking shows an item is moving. Mercari releases funds after the buyer rates the sale, and Instant Pay requires ID verification. Poshmark is the most forgiving for day-one sellers, releasing funds 3 days after delivery. Tight cash flow as a brand-new seller — Poshmark and Mercari will feel friendliest in month one.

The 1099-K Tax Angle

All four platforms issue a 1099-K once the IRS reporting threshold is crossed. That threshold has changed repeatedly in recent years, so don't trust any single number floating around online — verify the current IRS rule each tax season. What never changes: tax is owed on the sale date, not the payout date, and cost of goods, shipping, and fees are all deductible. Keeping a simple log of every sale and return matters, because a forced return in January for a December sale is exactly the kind of thing that scrambles self-reported numbers.

Cross-Listing Like A Pro

The honest answer for most resellers: list on more than one platform. Cross-listing multiplies exposure and lets the market decide where an item is worth the most. The catch is inventory discipline — if the same jacket sells on Poshmark and eBay an hour apart, one sale has to get cancelled, and repeated cancellations trigger Mercari's 5% fee and hurt eBay's defect rate. A cross-listing tool or a strict spreadsheet, plus delisting immediately after a sale, keeps this from becoming a problem. Treat cross-listing as a discovery mechanism — after a few weeks it becomes obvious which platform sells specific inventory fastest, and where the best pieces should go first.

FAQ

Which selling platform has the lowest fees in 2026?
Mercari and Depop at a flat 10%. Poshmark is the priciest at 20% over $15 (or $2.95 under $15). eBay sits in the middle at 13.6% plus a per-order fee.
Which app pays sellers the fastest?
eBay for established sellers (1–2 days to available funds). Mercari and Poshmark ~3 days after delivery. Depop 2–10 working days, longer for new sellers.
What is the Poshmark 5-pound shipping trap?
The flat-rate label covers up to 5 lbs. Bundles over 5 lbs trigger a $5–$10 overweight upgrade fee deducted from earnings.
Should I cross-list my items on multiple platforms?
Yes — it multiplies exposure. Just track inventory so the same item never sells twice, or face cancellation fees (Mercari) and defect-rate damage (eBay).
Do these platforms send a 1099-K?
Yes, all four issue a 1099-K once the IRS threshold is crossed. The threshold changes, so verify the current rule and log sale dates and costs.
Is Poshmark or eBay better for clothes?
Poshmark for mid-tier name-brand women's; eBay for designer, rare vintage and menswear.
Does Mercari charge to cash out?
Direct deposit is free. Instant Pay to a debit card costs $3 per transfer.
