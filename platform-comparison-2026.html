Poshmark vs Depop vs Mercari vs eBay: Which Platform Actually Pays You More? (The 2026 Matrix)

You just spent three hours at the thrift store and found a vintage Levi's jacket. It's beautiful. It's profitable. Then you hit the wall: where do I list this?

Ask any reseller group and you'll get a dozen loud opinions. "eBay has the traffic!" "Poshmark buyers pay more!" "Mercari is easiest!" But almost nobody does the actual math. The truth is there is no single "best" platform — there is only the best platform for that specific item, at that price, at that weight.

We pulled the official 2026 fee schedules, shipping policies and return rules from all four platforms, then ran real items through every single one. Welcome to The Reseller's Matrix.

The TL;DR: Heavy items (shoes, electronics) win on Mercari or eBay. Trendy women's vintage wins on Poshmark despite the 20% fee. Y2K streetwear wins on Depop. But read the shipping traps below before you list a single item — the trap, not the base fee, is usually what kills your profit.

The Bundle Economy: Who Actually Profits?

Bundles are where resellers quietly win or lose the month. On Poshmark, bundles are the whole culture — buyers expect to combine items for one $6.49 label. That's great for moving volume, but remember the 5lb wall: a bundle that tips past 5 lbs triggers a $5–$10 fee deducted from your earnings. Weigh before you accept.

On eBay, combined shipping is your choice. You can offer calculated shipping that automatically discounts when a buyer adds items, protecting your margin. On Mercari, bundles are manual — buyers ask, you adjust the price, and you ship one heavier package using a heavier prepaid label. On Depop, bundles are informal; you simply agree on a price and ship it yourself. The platform that rewards bundling most is Poshmark; the platform that punishes a heavy bundle most is also Poshmark. Know which side of the 5lb line your closet lives on.

Round 2: The Shipping Nightmares

Poshmark: The 5-Pound Bundle Trap

Poshmark's flat $6.49 buyer shipping and prepaid label are genuinely simple — until a buyer bundles. If two pairs of jeans push the package to 5.1 lbs, the flat label is void and Poshmark deducts a $5 overweight upgrade fee straight from your earnings. At 10.1 lbs it's $10. Always weigh before you approve a bundle.

Mercari: The 100lb Heavyweight Champion

Mercari's prepaid labels support packages up to 100 pounds — unbeatable for shoes, electronics and car parts. Just weigh accurately: underpaid-label overage fees come out of your balance.

Depop & eBay: Calculated Chaos

Depop leaves the shipping math entirely to you. eBay's calculated shipping is accurate, but a missed handling-time deadline spikes your Late Shipment Rate and risks your seller standing.

New-Seller Reality: Holds, Reserves & Verification

Brand-new accounts are treated as fraud risk everywhere, but the severity differs. Depop is the strictest: expect a 21–30 day rolling reserve on funds while you build history. eBay may place payment holds on new or below-standard accounts and can hold funds until tracking shows an item is on its way. Mercari releases funds after the buyer rates you, and Instant Pay requires ID verification. Poshmark is the most forgiving for day-one sellers, releasing funds 3 days after delivery. If cash flow is tight and you're brand new, Poshmark and Mercari will feel friendliest in your first month.

The 1099-K Tax Angle

All four platforms issue a 1099-K once you cross the IRS reporting threshold. That threshold has changed repeatedly in recent years, so don't trust any single number you read — verify the current IRS rule each tax season. What never changes: you owe tax on the sale date, not the payout date, and you can deduct cost of goods, shipping and fees. Keep a simple log of every sale and every return, because a forced return in January for a December sale is exactly the kind of thing that scrambles self-reported numbers.

Cross-Listing Like A Pro

The honest answer for most resellers: list on more than one platform. Cross-listing multiplies your exposure and lets the market tell you where an item is worth the most. The catch is inventory discipline — if the same jacket sells on Poshmark and eBay an hour apart, you're forced to cancel one, and repeated cancellations trigger Mercari's 5% fee and hurt your eBay defect rate. Use a cross-listing tool or a strict spreadsheet, and delist immediately after a sale. Treat cross-listing as a discovery mechanism: after a few weeks you'll see which platform sells your specific inventory fastest, and you'll know where to put your best pieces first.

FAQ

Which selling platform has the lowest fees in 2026?
Mercari and Depop at a flat 10%. Poshmark is the priciest at 20% over $15 (or $2.95 under $15). eBay sits in the middle at 13.6% plus a per-order fee.
Which app pays sellers the fastest?
eBay for established sellers (1–2 days to available funds). Mercari and Poshmark ~3 days after delivery. Depop 2–10 working days, longer for new sellers.
What is the Poshmark 5-pound shipping trap?
The flat-rate label covers up to 5 lbs. Bundles over 5 lbs trigger a $5–$10 overweight upgrade fee deducted from your earnings.
Should I cross-list my items on multiple platforms?
Yes — it multiplies exposure. Just track inventory so the same item never sells twice, or you'll face cancellation fees (Mercari) and defect-rate damage (eBay).
Do these platforms send a 1099-K?
Yes, all four issue a 1099-K once you cross the IRS threshold. The threshold changes, so verify the current rule and log your sale dates and costs.
Is Poshmark or eBay better for clothes?
Poshmark for mid-tier name-brand women's; eBay for designer, rare vintage and menswear.
Does Mercari charge to cash out?
Direct deposit is free. Instant Pay to a debit card costs $3 per transfer.
