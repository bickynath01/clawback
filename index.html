    STRAIGHT ANSWERS

    Is CLAWBACK free, and is my data safe?

    Yes, completely free with no signup and no login. Every number is calculated inside your own browser, so your sale price and return details are never sent to any server. Close the tab and nothing is stored anywhere.

    Do eBay, Poshmark, Mercari or Depop charge a "return fee"?

    None of them has a line item called a return fee. The real loss on a forced return comes from three places the platforms don't label: selling fees they don't fully refund, return shipping they make you pay, and the drop in the item's value when it comes back damaged or swapped. CLAWBACK adds those three up into one dollar figure — which is exactly the number every other calculator leaves out.

    On eBay, do I get my fees back when an item is returned?

    It depends on the reason. If the buyer simply changed their mind or the item didn't fit, eBay credits the final value fee and the per-order fee. If the return was "not as described", damaged, or the wrong item, eBay credits the final value percentage but keeps the $0.30–$0.40 per-order fee. If eBay stepped in and ruled against you, or you refunded the buyer outside of eBay, you get no credit at all. The "what happened" selector above runs this exact rule.

    Who pays the return shipping?

    On eBay the seller pays return shipping for "not as described", damaged, or wrong-item claims. On Mercari the buyer gets a free prepaid label for items up to 50 lbs, but the seller must pay the return label for anything over 50 lbs (and provide it within 3 days or the order is canceled). Poshmark and Depop generally provide a prepaid return label on approved returns.

    Can I write off return losses on my taxes?

    Generally yes — returned, damaged, or unsellable inventory can reduce your taxable income as a cost-of-goods adjustment. The key is documentation: after each loss, use the "copy for taxes" line to keep a clean one-line record, so January is painless. Then confirm the exact treatment with a tax professional.

    Does Whatnot really take 8%, or is it more?

    Whatnot advertises 8% commission, but payment processing is 2.9% + $0.30 on the gross order value — item + buyer shipping + buyer sales tax — money you never pocket. On a $100 card with $8 shipping and $5 tax, the real take is ≈ 12.7% before carrier weight clawbacks. Eligible categories get 0% commission on the portion above $1,500. The calculator shows your true "bite ratio" instantly.

    Does Whatnot charge VAT on fees?

    Yes — for UK and EU sellers. Whatnot adds 20% VAT on top of both the commission and the payment processing fee (a £3.33 commission becomes £4.00 after VAT). The calculator applies VAT automatically when you select the UK or EU region. US sellers pay no VAT on fees.

    Does Vinted charge sellers any fees?

    No. Vinted charges $0 seller fees in 2026 — no commission, no processing, no listing fee. The buyer pays a $0.70 + 5% protection fee plus shipping, and you keep 100% of your listed price. That's why Vinted is the anchor in our 6-platform comparison. Optional paid boosts are the only seller cost.
