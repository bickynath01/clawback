What Do I Lose on a Poshmark Damaged Return? (The 72-Hour Breakdown)

You lose three things: the drop in the item's value when it comes back worse, any overweight shipping fee you paid (the $5–$10 bundle trap), and the new Texas Seller Fee Tax if you ship from Texas. You do not lose the 20% commission — Poshmark reverses it when a return is approved. The buyer has 72 hours to open a case, you have 24 hours to respond, and the damage happens somewhere in between. This is the full 2026 picture.

The TL;DR for impatient sellers: A $50 dress that comes back stained and now worth $20 costs you $30 in value drop. The 20% commission comes back, the buyer's $6.49 shipping stays with Poshmark, and you eat the damage. That's the real cost most Poshmark sellers never add up.

First — Bust The Myth About Commission

Almost every reseller blog and Facebook group repeats the same line: "Poshmark keeps the 20% on returns." That's wrong in 2026. When a return is approved and the buyer is refunded, Poshmark reverses the commission — both the 20% on sales over $15 and the $2.95 flat fee on sales under $15. Unlike eBay, there's no per-order fee that stays gone.

So what do you actually lose? Three things, all of which happen outside the commission line: the item's value drop, the overweight bundle fee (if any), and — since October 1, 2025 — the Texas Seller Fee Tax on the commission that was originally deducted.

The 72-Hour Time Bomb: Hour by Hour

Poshmark's return window is brutal because it's so short. Most sellers don't realize how fast the clock moves. Here's exactly what happens, hour by hour, when a buyer claims an item is damaged or not as described:

THE 3-DAY COUNTDOWN

DAY 0
Delivery confirmed by USPS. The 3-day clock starts. The buyer has until this moment plus 72 hours to open a case. Until then, the item sits in their house and you have no idea what's happening.
DAY 1-3
Buyer opens "Order Not as Described" case. They must upload clear, well-lit photos (natural lighting, JPG only — Poshmark's system doesn't accept HEIC files). They can also include video for electronics.
+24 HRS
Your response window. You have 24 hours to respond to the case. You can accept the return, offer a partial refund, or reject it. If you don't respond, Poshmark's team takes over and decides for you.
DAY 4-7
Poshmark reviews. Their team evaluates the evidence. If they side with the buyer, a prepaid return label is generated. The buyer ships the item back to you.
DAY 8+
You receive the item. This is where you find out what you actually lost. Is it still wearable? Stained? Swapped? This is also where you appeal if the item came back worse than it left.

The entire dance happens in under two weeks. And the critical 24-hour seller response window is where most damaged returns are decided. If you're asleep at the wheel, Poshmark decides for you — and they tend to side with whoever has better evidence.

Commission DOES Come Back (The Myth, Busted)

Let's be explicit, because this is where most reseller content is wrong: when Poshmark approves a return and refunds the buyer, they reverse the commission on the refunded amount. That means:

  • Sales over $15: The 20% commission is credited back to you.
  • Sales under $15: The $2.95 flat fee is credited back to you.
  • No per-order fee: Unlike eBay's $0.30/$0.40 per-order charge that stays gone, Poshmark has no equivalent. You get a clean reversal.

This is one of the reasons Poshmark can be more seller-friendly than eBay for high-value items. On a $200 coat, eBay keeps $0.40 of the per-order fee plus any Very High INAD penalty exposure. Poshmark reverses everything on the commission side. The tradeoff is that Poshmark's flat $6.49 buyer-paid shipping means the buyer always covers outbound shipping — so your cost exposure starts after the sale.

The 5lb Bundle Trap: Where Returns Get Expensive

This is the part almost nobody talks about. Poshmark's flat-rate $6.49 shipping covers packages up to 5 pounds. When a buyer bundles multiple items and the package tips past 5 lbs, Poshmark makes you generate an overweight label upgrade, and the fee comes out of your earnings:

Bundle weightUpgrade feeWhen this bites
Up to 5 lbs$0Normal bundle
5.01–10 lbs$5.00Two pairs of jeans, or shoes + clothing
10.01–15 lbs$10.00Heavy winter coat bundle

Now here's the damaged-return wrinkle: if you paid a $5 or $10 overweight fee on the outbound shipment and the item comes back damaged, you've already spent that money and can't get it back. The upgrade fee is deducted at the time of label generation, and it's gone even when the sale reverses. On a $120 coat bundle that tips to 6 lbs, your total loss on a damaged return becomes $56.58 — not $51.58. The $5 overweight fee is a permanent sunk cost.

The Texas Seller Fee Tax (New October 2025)

Effective October 1, 2025, Poshmark began collecting an additional tax on sellers based in Texas. It's 8.25% (6.25% state + ~2% local) applied to 80% of the seller fee, not the full sale. On a $100 sale:

  • Poshmark's commission: $20 (20%)
  • Taxable portion: $16 (80% of $20)
  • Texas tax: $1.32 ($16 × 8.25%)

This tax is deducted at the time of sale and shows up separately in your My Sales Report under "Texas Seller Fee Tax." When a return reverses the commission, the Texas tax is also reversed — but you've already been out that cash for the duration of the return process. For high-volume Texas sellers, this tax compounds across hundreds of transactions.

Seel Worry-Free Returns: When You Lose $0

Here's the most important change on Poshmark in years — and almost no reseller has internalized it yet. Seel Worry-Free Returns is an optional add-on buyers can purchase at checkout. If they do, and they return the item for fit or change-of-mind reasons:

  • Seel pays the refund, not you. Your earnings stay exactly where they were.
  • The item goes to Seel's warehouse, not back to you. You don't have to receive it, inspect it, or relist it.
  • Your payout timing doesn't change. You get paid on the standard 3-day-after-acceptance schedule.
  • You can't opt out. Sellers don't control whether buyers add Seel protection.
What this means for you: A buyer adding Seel protection on a $60 dress is effectively buying insurance against their fit uncertainty — and it costs you nothing. The only return types that still hurt you are "not as described" cases handled by Poshmark itself. Seel returns are a gift; Poshmark returns are a fight.

How To Fight Back: The Photo Evidence Method

The sellers who win Poshmark return cases share one habit: they photograph every item before shipping. Front, back, collar, inside label, any flaws, and serial numbers for high-value items. Natural lighting, JPG format (Poshmark's system rejects HEIC).

When a buyer claims "damaged" or "not as described," you upload your pre-shipment photos alongside whatever they sent. If your photos show the item in perfect condition and theirs show damage, you have a strong case. If they return the item and it's worse than it left, photograph it again immediately — side-by-side with your pre-shipment shots — and open a case with Poshmark support. Sellers who itemize with photos win. Sellers who send paragraphs of angry text don't.

We have a full walkthrough of the photo-evidence method in how to win a return dispute. The short version: one photo per angle, timestamped, uploaded within the 24-hour seller response window.

FAQ

What do I lose on a Poshmark damaged return?
The commission is reversed, so you don't lose the 20%. You lose the value drop (item worth less when it comes back), any overweight bundle fee you paid, and the Texas Seller Fee Tax if you ship from Texas.
Does Poshmark refund the 20% commission on a return?
Yes. When a return is approved and the buyer is refunded, Poshmark reverses the 20% commission (or $2.95 for sub-$15 sales). Unlike eBay, Poshmark has no per-order fee that stays gone.
What is the 3-day rule on Poshmark returns?
Buyers have 3 days from delivery to open a case for "not as described." After that, no return can be filed. Sellers then have 24 hours to respond to any opened case.
Who pays return shipping on Poshmark?
Poshmark provides a prepaid return label for approved returns. The label cost is absorbed by the platform, not you. But you lose the value of the item if it returns damaged, and any overweight bundle fee you paid outbound is a sunk cost.
Does Poshmark side with the buyer or seller?
Whoever has better evidence. Clear pre-shipment photos with serial numbers and documented flaws tend to go to the seller. Buyer-only claims with weak evidence tend to go to the buyer.
Does Seel Worry-Free Returns hurt Poshmark sellers?
No. Seel returns for fit or change-of-mind reasons cost sellers nothing. Seel pays the refund, the item goes to Seel's warehouse, and your earnings stay put.
What is the Texas Seller Fee Tax on Poshmark?
Effective October 1, 2025, Texas sellers pay 8.25% tax on 80% of the commission. On a $100 sale, that's about $1.32. It's deducted at sale time and reversed on returns.
Can I write off Poshmark return losses on my taxes?
Yes. Value drop on damaged returns, overweight bundle fees, and any absorbed return shipping are all deductible as ordinary business expenses on Schedule C.
