Are Depop Fees Refunded When I Refund a Buyer?

Mostly — but not all of it. On a Depop refund, the Depop selling fee is typically returned, while the payment-processing fee often isn't. Here's the real picture.

What gets returned, what doesn't

Depop charges a selling fee (commonly 10%) plus payment processing. When you fully refund a buyer, the Depop selling fee is generally credited back to you — but the payment-processing portion frequently stays with the processor. Add any return shipping or a drop in the item's value, and that's your actual loss.

Policies change, so verify the current Depop terms — and use the calculator for your exact number.

Worked example

You sold an item for $40 and refunded the buyer. The Depop fee is returned, but the payment-processing fee (roughly $1.50) isn't — and the item returns in the same condition. Your loss: about $1.50 plus any shipping you covered. Small here, but it compounds across returns.
