Are Depop Fees Refunded When You Refund a Buyer?

If you just refunded a Depop buyer and your first thought was "Did I just lose all the fees too?", there is good news: Depop's current policy says the fees are reversed when a sale is refunded. That includes the selling fee where one applies, payment-processing fees, and boosting fees.

Seller relief, first: Depop's current Seller fees and charges policy says that when a sale is refunded, all fees are reversed — selling fees, payment-processing fees and boosting fees. So, Does Depop Refund the Fees or Not?

Yes. This is one of those Depop questions where old forum answers can make the situation sound worse than it is.

Depop's current fee policy says:

  • Selling fees: reversed when the sale is refunded, where a selling fee applied.
  • Payment-processing fees: reversed when the sale is refunded.
  • Boosting fees: reversed when the sale is refunded.

For sellers using Depop Payments, Depop says the reversal happens automatically. citeturn0search1turn0search0

So if you are searching "does Depop refund fees on returns?", "does Depop refund the processing fee?", or "I refunded a buyer on Depop, do I get the fees back?", the current answer is reassuring: the fees themselves are not supposed to become a permanent loss simply because you refunded the sale.

Yes — according to Depop's current policy.

This is the part that needs the clearest correction because older reseller content often says the opposite. Depop's current Seller fees and charges page specifically states that when a sale is refunded, all fees, including payment-processing fees, are reversed too. citeturn0search1

I Refunded a Depop Buyer — What Happens to My Money?

Think of the refund as two separate events:

  1. The buyer gets the refund.
  2. The fees associated with the refunded sale are reversed.

For a Depop Payments transaction, the refund is taken from your available Depop Balance or, if your balance is not enough, from your connected top-up card. Depop's refund instructions say the money is returned to the buyer's original payment method. citeturn1search0turn1search2

That means a seller can temporarily see a lower or even negative balance while the refund is being funded. That is a cash-flow issue, not evidence that Depop has permanently kept the transaction fees.

What If I Don't See My Depop Fee Refund?

Don't panic and don't refund the buyer a second time just because the fee line is confusing.

Use this checklist:

  1. Confirm the buyer refund actually processed. Check the order in your Depop app.
  2. Open the sale receipt. Depop says sellers can review the fee breakdown from their sold-item receipts. citeturn0search2
  3. Check Depop Balance. Depop Balance shows sales and refunds and the amount available for payout. citeturn1search2
  4. Look for the fee reversal. The relevant selling, processing and/or boosting fee should be reversed as part of the refunded transaction under the current policy.
  5. If you used PayPal outside the UK, US or Australia, follow Depop's separate support process. Depop says sellers using PayPal in those markets need to contact Support for a selling-fee refund and provide the requested proof. citeturn0search1turn1search1
  6. If the refund processed but the fee reversal is missing, contact Depop Support. Include the transaction receipt and refund confirmation so Support can identify the sale.
Most important: Don't calculate your final loss from the original sale receipt alone. After a refund, check the updated transaction / Depop Balance because the fee reversals are part of the final picture.

What If I Used PayPal?

This is the one case where the "it happens automatically" answer needs a qualification.

Depop says that if you sold through PayPal and are outside the UK, US or Australia, you need to contact Depop Support for a refund of the Depop selling fee. Depop asks for information including the email linked to your account, your Depop @username, the buyer's @username, and proof of the full refund in PayPal. citeturn1search1turn0search1

What If I Give a Partial Refund?

A partial refund is different from a full refund because the buyer keeps the item and receives only part of their payment back.

Depop's seller refund instructions distinguish a partial refund from a full refund: a partial refund can be used when the buyer keeps the item and receives some money back. citeturn1search0

Depop's fee policy says that if a sale is refunded, all fees are reversed. However, because a partial refund only returns part of the buyer's payment, do not promise yourself a particular dollar-for-dollar fee reversal without checking the actual transaction receipt. citeturn0search1

Seller-safe answer: for a partial refund, check the transaction after it processes rather than assuming the same fee calculation as a full refund. The receipt is the best way to confirm what Depop actually reversed.

So What Do I Actually Lose on a Depop Refund?

This is the question sellers usually care about more than the wording of the fee policy.

For a refunded order, separate your costs into two buckets:

Bucket 1 — Depop transaction fees

  • Selling fee, where applicable
  • Payment-processing fee
  • Boosting fee, where applicable

Current Depop policy says these are reversed when the sale is refunded. citeturn0search1turn0search0

Bucket 2 — Other seller costs

  • Shipping or return-label costs
  • Packaging already used
  • Time spent handling the return
  • Potential loss in item value if the item comes back damaged or altered

Those are not automatically transformed into "fee refunds." They need to be considered separately when you calculate your actual business loss.

This is the relief point: If you were worried that Depop's selling, processing and boosting fees would simply disappear forever after you refunded the buyer, the current Depop policy says otherwise. The fee portion is reversed. Your remaining loss, if any, comes from the other costs and circumstances of the return.
