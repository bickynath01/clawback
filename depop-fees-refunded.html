Are Depop Fees Refunded? What Sellers Keep (2026)

Quick answer: Yes — when a Depop Payments sale is refunded, Depop reverses the applicable selling fee (where it applies), the payment-processing fee and the Boosting fee automatically. PayPal sellers outside the UK, US and Australia must contact Depop Support to request the selling-fee refund. But a fee reversal is not a zero-loss refund: return postage, packaging and any drop in the returned item's resale value can still stay with you.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

You refund a Depop buyer. The sale is reversed, but what happens to the fees you paid?

It is a fair question. You might have paid payment-processing fees, been charged for Boosting, or paid shipping costs before the refund happened. You need to know which amounts come back and which expenses may still leave you out of pocket.

The short answer: Depop says applicable selling, payment-processing and Boosting fees are reversed when a sale is refunded through Depop Payments. PayPal transactions can follow a different process, particularly for the Depop selling-fee refund in markets where that fee applies. But a fee reversal does not automatically make the whole transaction loss-free. Shipping, return postage, packaging and a returned item's reduced resale value are separate financial issues.

The short answer: does Depop refund fees?

If the sale used Depop Payments

Depop's current seller-fee policy says that when a sale is refunded, the applicable Depop fees are reversed. These can include:

  • Selling fees, where applicable
  • Payment-processing fees
  • Boosting fees, where applicable

The reversal is handled as part of the refund process. Check the updated transaction to confirm how the amounts were adjusted.

If the sale used PayPal

The process is different. For sellers outside the UK, US and Australia, Depop says the seller can request a refund of the applicable Depop selling fee by contacting Support and providing proof of the completed refund.

PayPal's own processing fees are separate from Depop's selling fee. Do not assume that a Depop fee reversal means every PayPal charge or seller-paid expense is also returned.

The key distinction: check which payment method was used, which fee was charged, and what the updated transaction actually shows.

Does Depop refund the payment-processing fee?

For Depop Payments, Depop's current seller-fee policy includes payment-processing fees among the fees reversed when a sale is refunded.

That matters because processing fees can be visible on the original sale even when the seller has no selling fee. A 0% selling fee does not mean that no processing fee was charged.

For example, a US seller might see a processing charge on a completed sale and then refund the buyer. The seller should review the updated transaction to confirm the applicable fee reversal rather than treating the original processing deduction as a permanent loss.

If the sale used PayPal, check the PayPal transaction separately. Depop's fee-reversal policy should not be treated as a guarantee that PayPal's own processing charge will be handled in the same way.

Does Depop refund the 10% selling fee?

Where the 10% selling fee applies, Depop says the applicable selling fee is reversed when a sale is refunded, subject to the relevant process.

However, do not assume that every Depop seller paid a 10% fee in the first place. Under the current fee structure, sellers in the US, UK and Australia have a 0% Depop selling fee. Payment-processing and qualifying Boosting fees can still apply.

For PayPal sellers outside the UK, US and Australia, Depop's instructions say to contact Support to request the applicable selling-fee refund and provide proof that the buyer's refund was completed.

For the standard fee rates and calculation bases, see Depop Fees Explained. This article focuses on what happens after a refund.

Does Depop refund Boosting fees?

Depop's current Boosted Listings policy says the Boosting fee is refunded when the buyer is refunded.

If the original sale included a Boosting fee, check the updated transaction after the refund. Do not assume that the original Boosting deduction is the final financial result.

The Boosting fee and other seller costs are separate questions. A reversed Boosting fee does not, by itself, reimburse shipping, packaging or any loss in the value of a returned item.

Does a partial refund also reverse fees?

A partial refund is not the same transaction as a full refund. Depop supports partial refunds, and its refund-support process addresses fee refunds after a partial refund.

Do not assume that a partial refund produces a fixed, proportional fee reversal in every case. The final adjustment depends on the transaction and the applicable process.

After a partial refund:

  1. Open the updated transaction.
  2. Check the amount returned to the buyer.
  3. Review any fee adjustment or reversal shown.
  4. Compare the resulting transaction with the original receipt.
  5. Contact Depop Support if the completed adjustment does not match the applicable policy.

The important thing is to use the completed transaction rather than estimating the fee reversal from the partial-refund amount alone.

Why does my Depop Balance look different after a refund?

A refund changes the transaction, so your current balance may not match what you expected from the original sale.

For Depop Payments, Depop's guidance explains that the refund is taken from your available Depop Balance. If the balance is insufficient, a connected top-up card may be used to fund the refund.

Keep the stages separate:

Original sale→Buyer refund→Applicable fee reversals→Updated balance

Do not compare only the original payout with the refund sent to the buyer. Review the updated transaction and any related balance adjustments to understand the final position.

How do I check whether Depop refunded my fees?

Start with the transaction itself.

  1. Confirm that the refund completed. A refund that has not finished processing may not yet show the final adjustment.
  2. Open your Depop transaction activity. For Depop Payments, Depop's current instructions direct sellers to their account's balance and latest transactions.
  3. Find the original sale. Identify the original selling, processing and Boosting fees, where applicable.
  4. Look for the refund and fee reversal. Check the updated transaction rather than relying on the original receipt alone.
  5. Check the payment method. If you used PayPal, review PayPal's transaction activity as well.
  6. Contact Support if something is missing. For a PayPal selling-fee refund where Depop requires a request, follow the current instructions and provide proof of the completed buyer refund.

Are shipping costs refunded when you refund a Depop buyer?

Do not treat shipping as automatically included in the phrase "fee refund."

Platform fees and shipping expenses are different things. A reversal of a Depop selling, processing or Boosting fee does not automatically mean that every postage expense is reimbursed.

Depending on the transaction, the seller may have already paid for the original label, return postage or packaging. The applicable shipping arrangement and any reimbursement need to be checked separately.

For detailed guidance on shipping methods, labels and seller-side shipping costs, see Depop Shipping Explained.

What if the item comes back damaged—or never comes back?

This is where the difference between a fee refund and a seller's actual loss becomes important.

Imagine a $100 item is refunded. The applicable Depop fees are reversed, but the item comes back damaged and is now worth less than it was before the sale.

The seller's remaining loss may include:

  • Return postage paid by the seller
  • Packaging or other expenses that cannot be recovered
  • The difference between the item's previous resale value and its current recoverable value

If the item never comes back, the seller may also face an unrecovered-item loss, depending on the circumstances and any confirmed reimbursement.

These losses are not automatically erased by a platform-fee reversal. Equally, do not count a reversed fee as a permanent loss if the completed transaction shows that the fee was returned.

Use the right calculation for your situation

There are two separate questions:

1. Were the Depop fees reversed? Check the transaction and the relevant fee policy. This tells you whether a selling, processing or Boosting fee remains charged.

2. How much did the refund actually cost me? Account for seller-paid shipping, return costs, packaging, unrecovered items and any reduction in the item's resale value. Subtract confirmed reimbursements and do not count reversed fees as permanent losses.

Frequently asked questions

Does Depop refund fees when you refund a buyer?

For Depop Payments, Depop says applicable selling, payment-processing and Boosting fees are reversed when a sale is refunded. PayPal transactions can follow a different process for the Depop selling-fee refund.

Does Depop refund the payment-processing fee?

Depop's current policy says the applicable Depop Payments processing fee is reversed when the sale is refunded. PayPal's own processing fees are governed separately by PayPal's rules.

Does Depop refund the 10% selling fee?

Where the 10% selling fee applies, Depop says the applicable fee is reversed under its refund process. Sellers in the US, UK and Australia currently have no Depop selling fee under the current policy.

Does Depop refund Boosting fees?

Depop's current Boosted Listings policy says the Boosting fee is refunded when the buyer is refunded. Check the updated transaction to confirm the adjustment.

Do I need to request my Depop fee refund?

For Depop Payments, the applicable fee reversal is handled automatically when the refund is processed. PayPal sellers outside the UK, US and Australia may need to contact Depop Support to request the applicable Depop selling-fee refund.

Does a partial refund also reverse fees?

Depop supports partial refunds and its Support process addresses fee refunds after partial refunds. Check the completed transaction rather than assuming a fixed fee amount will be reversed.

Why does my Depop Balance look lower after a refund?

A refund changes your transaction and may be funded from your available balance or a connected top-up card if your balance is insufficient. Review the updated transaction and related balance activity.

Does Depop refund shipping costs too?

Do not assume that a platform-fee reversal reimburses shipping or return postage. Check the applicable shipping arrangement and any confirmed reimbursement separately.

What if the buyer got a refund but never returned my item?

Check the return status, relevant transaction and any Support decision. If the item has not been recovered, include its unrecovered value in your loss assessment rather than assuming the fee reversal covers it.

What if the returned item is damaged?

Record the item's condition, preserve relevant evidence and estimate its current recoverable resale value. Any loss in item value is separate from the reversal of Depop fees.

Where can I see whether the fees were refunded?

Start with the updated transaction in your Depop account. If you used PayPal, also review the PayPal activity and follow Depop's current Support process where a selling-fee refund request is required.

The bottom line

Depop's current policy says applicable selling, processing and Boosting fees are reversed when a sale is refunded through Depop Payments. PayPal transactions can require a different process, particularly for the Depop selling-fee refund outside the UK, US and Australia.

But a fee refund is not the same as a zero-loss refund. Shipping, return postage, packaging, unrecovered items and reduced resale value can still affect the seller's final position.

Check the completed transaction, separate platform fees from other expenses, and calculate only the costs that remain after confirmed reversals and reimbursements.
