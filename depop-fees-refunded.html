Are Depop Fees Refunded When I Refund a Buyer? (The 2026 Receipts)

Yes. If the buyer paid through Depop Payments, Depop reverses all fees when you refund — the 10% selling fee, the payments processing fee, even boosting fees — automatically, the moment the refund processes. If you sold via PayPal, the Depop selling fee comes back only after you raise a support ticket, and PayPal's own processing fee usually stays. Either way, your real leftover loss is return shipping and any drop in the item's value.

That is the direct answer to the question half the reseller forums are still getting wrong. The rest of this guide shows you the receipts at three price tiers, the PayPal catch, the boosting-fee rule most sellers miss, the exact menu paths to verify every reversed fee in your own account, and why the eBay × Depop news of the last year makes this the most important refund article on the internet right now.

The myth we are killing today: half the reseller blogs still claim "the processing fee never comes back on Depop." That was never the Depop Payments rule — it describes PayPal's processing fee and other platforms' fixed fees (eBay keeps its $0.30 per-order fee on refunds, as we showed in does eBay refund the per-order fee?). Know which fee, which rail, which rule.

What Depop's Official Policy Actually Says

Depop's help center is unusually clear here: "If your sale is refunded, all fees (the selling fee, payments processing fees and boosting fees) will be reversed too. This happens automatically for transactions using Depop Payments." For PayPal sellers outside the UK, US or AUS, the selling fee is refunded after raising a ticket with proof of the refund. The processing fee charged by PayPal is separate and does not come back.

This is a newer policy than most people realize. For years, older third-party articles (and a lot of Reddit threads from 2022–2024) described the old Depop-Stripe-UK rail, where the 2.9% processing charge stayed. Since Depop Payments rolled out and harmonized under eBay's ownership, the rule changed — and most content has not caught up. Always trust the official help page over a 2023 Reddit comment.

The Boosting Fee Gotcha (The One Most Sellers Miss)

Here is the clause almost nobody quotes from the help page: "If your sale is refunded, all fees (the selling fee, payments processing fees and boosting fees) will be reversed too." That last bit matters, because boosted items can carry an extra 8% on top of the selling fee. If you spent $3.20 boosting a $40 jacket and the buyer returns it, Depop gives you that $3.20 back automatically on Depop Payments. That is a genuinely generous policy — no other platform we track (eBay's Promoted Listings, Poshmark, Mercari) fully reverses ad spend on refunds.

The practical rule: if you are refunding a boosted item, check Latest Transactions for the specific boosting line. If it has not reversed within 48 hours of the refund confirming, raise a support ticket with the transaction ID — it should have, but glitches happen.

Full Refund vs Partial Refund vs Return: Which Hurts Least?

Full refund (Depop Payments): buyer gets everything back; every fee line reverses. Your loss = return shipping (if any) plus value drop.

Partial refund: the buyer keeps the item and gets some money back — Depop's page confirms partials are agreed between buyer and seller and require no return. The official page does not spell out proportional fee reversals on partials, so check your transaction breakdown afterwards. Partials are usually the cheapest peace: no return label, no value drop, no relist.

Return + refund: on a not-as-described dispute, US buyers get a prepaid return label; buyers outside the US arrange tracked shipping and Depop reimburses them up to $20/£20. Items must go back within 7 days of the agreed return. Your exposure here is time and the item's condition on arrival — photograph everything before and after, as we preach in Depop refund fees.

Buyer's Fault vs Seller's Fault vs Depop Stepped In

Changed mind / doesn't fit: Depop Protection does not cover remorse. The official policy lists what is covered: lost and damaged items, incorrect sizes, wrong colors, counterfeits, severe damage, major wear not detailed, incorrect items, missing bundle items. Remorse, fit, smell, and "material that looks different" are not covered — so you are not obligated to refund. If you choose to anyway, fees still reverse on Depop Payments.

Not as described / never arrived: the buyer can raise an issue within 30 days. You get 48 hours to respond with a solution; silence auto-escalates to Depop, whose decision is final — including refunding without a return. That auto-escalation is a real risk: a single non-response can flip a $40 jacket into a total loss.

Depop stepped in: once escalated, outcomes follow their dispute review. If the buyer's claim is upheld without a return, you lose the item, the sale, and the return shipping — but you still get the fees back. The calculator below helps you model that exact scenario.

How To Refund A Buyer (Exact Steps, iOS + Android)

REFUND + FEE REVERSAL WALKTHROUGH

  1. Open the app. iOS: tap My Depop → Sold → All. Android: tap Selling Hub → Sold items.
  2. Select the order and tap Refund sale. For a partial, switch to the partial tab and enter the amount.
  3. Confirm. If the buyer paid with Depop Payments, the refund is deducted from your Depop Balance — or charged to your connected top-up card if the balance is too low.
  4. Verify the reversal. Go to My Depop → My Account → Depop Balance → scroll to Latest Transactions. You should see the original fee lines flip back within the same day.

PayPal-sold items are refunded through PayPal directly (desktop only), and then you file a support ticket to get the Depop selling fee back. It is slower but it still works — just keep the screenshots.

How Long Does A Depop Refund Take?

Refunds issued through Depop Payments typically process within a few hours and the fee reversals appear in Latest Transactions the same day. Bank-to-bank transfers can take 1 to 3 business days for the buyer to see the funds in their account. PayPal refunds usually process in minutes on PayPal's side but can take up to 5 business days to reach the buyer's card.

The "when will my money come through" anxiety is real — we broke down the full payout timeline (including the 21-day new-seller reserve) in when do I get paid on Depop? Refunds do not extend your payout window, but a dispute does freeze funds until resolved.

Conclusion: Price The Risk, Not The Myth

The 10% commission, the processing fee, the boosting fee — on Depop Payments, they all come back when you refund. What does not come back is the return label, the item's value after a return, and your 48 hours of response time if a dispute opens. That is the real math. Price that into your listings, keep your Latest Transactions open after every refund, and treat the old "processing fee never comes back" myth the way it deserves: as outdated content that quietly cost you money.

FAQ

Does Depop refund the 10% selling fee when I refund a buyer?
Yes. Depop's official policy states that when a sale is refunded, the selling fee is reversed. With Depop Payments this happens automatically when the refund processes.
Does Depop refund the payment processing fee too?
With Depop Payments, yes — the payments processing fee is reversed along with the selling fee and boosting fees. With PayPal, only the Depop selling fee is refunded via a support ticket; PayPal's own processing fee usually stays.
Do I get fees back on a partial refund on Depop?
Depop's help page confirms full-refund reversals but does not spell out partial refunds. Check the transaction breakdown in your Depop Balance after issuing a partial refund, since partials do not require the buyer to return the item.
How do I get a Depop fee refund if I sold through PayPal?
Fill out Depop's contact form with your account email, your @username, the buyer's @username, and proof of the completed refund. Depop will refund the selling fee; the PayPal processing fee is charged separately by PayPal.
What happens to boosting fees if I refund a buyer?
Boosting fees are reversed too. Depop's policy lists the selling fee, payments processing fees and boosting fees as all being reversed when a sale is refunded. Verify the specific boosting line in Latest Transactions.
Does Depop charge a refund fee?
No. Depop does not charge a separate refund fee. It simply reverses the fees it already collected on the original sale. Your real leftover cost is return shipping and any value drop in the item.
How long does a Depop refund take to process?
Refunds issued through Depop Payments typically process within a few hours and the fee reversals appear in Latest Transactions the same day. Bank-to-bank transfers can take 1 to 3 business days for the buyer to see the funds.
What is a top-up card on Depop?
A top-up card is a connected card that lets you refund buyers even when your Depop Balance is too low. Depop requires you to add one before you can issue refunds.
Does refunding a buyer protect my seller standing?
Resolving issues quickly keeps disputes out of escalation. If you do not respond to a not-as-described issue within 48 hours it is automatically escalated to Depop, whose decision is final.
