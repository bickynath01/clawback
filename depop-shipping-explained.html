Depop Shipping Explained (2026): Labels, Costs, Free Shipping & What Sellers Actually Need to Know

You made the sale. Now you're looking at the sold order thinking: Where's my label? Who pays for this? Did I choose Depop Shipping or my own shipping? What happens if the package weighs more than I entered? And could I accidentally lose my seller protection?

Those are the shipping questions that actually matter. Depop shipping is fairly simple once you know which system your listing is using. The problems usually start when sellers assume one shipping method works like another, guess the package weight, ship to an address the buyer sent in messages, or treat a temporary shipping promotion as a permanent rule.

This guide focuses on what happens after a Depop sale, so you can get the item out the door without creating an avoidable shipping, tracking, cost or protection problem.

The 30-second answer

Before you print a label or take anything to the post office, check four things:

  • Which shipping method did the listing use?
  • What address is shown on the sold order?
  • What does the finished package actually weigh?
  • Are you still within Depop's shipping and protection deadlines?

If the listing uses Depop Shipping, use the label generated by Depop. If the listing uses Other shipping, you arrange your own tracked shipping and add the tracking information to the order. If you offered free shipping, don't assume the shipping cost disappeared. Depending on the promotion or listing setup, you may still need to account for the shipping cost when working out what the sale is really worth.

The easiest way to avoid most problems is simple:

Check→pack→weigh→label→track→keep proof

First: find out which shipping method the sale is using

This is the first thing to check because Depop Shipping and Other shipping are different workflows. A seller can remember choosing "shipping" when listing an item without realizing that the actual listing is set to one specific shipping method.

If the listing uses Depop Shipping

Depop generates the shipping label. In the US, Depop Shipping uses USPS. In the UK, Depop Shipping uses Evri. In Australia, current new listings use Depop Shipping through Australia Post. Depop Shipping is tracked, and when you choose that method, Depop says you must use the label it generated rather than replacing it with another shipping method.

If the listing uses Other shipping

You organize the shipping yourself. That means you buy your own tracked label, ship to the address shown on the Depop order, add the tracking information, and mark the order as shipped where required. You don't automatically receive a prepaid Depop label for an Other-shipping order.

The important rule: Don't decide which label to use after you've already packed the item. Check the shipping method on the sold order first.

"I sold something. Where is my Depop shipping label?"

If you expected a Depop label and can't find one, don't immediately assume the app is broken. First check the shipping method. If the order is using Depop Shipping, the label should be available through the sold order (Depop's current US and UK shipping instructions provide a QR code and printable label). If the order is set to Other, however, you won't automatically receive a prepaid Depop label. You're responsible for arranging the shipment yourself.

This is one of the recurring points of confusion in seller discussions: someone expects a QR code because they thought the listing was using Depop Shipping, then discovers the listing was actually configured for manual shipping.

If Depop says "Other Shipping," what do I do?

It's straightforward. Your workflow is:

  1. Pack the item.
  2. Weigh the finished package.
  3. Buy a tracked shipping label.
  4. Ship it to the address shown on the Depop receipt.
  5. Add the tracking number to the order.
  6. Keep your proof of shipment.

The important word is tracked. If you're arranging your own shipping, don't save a couple of dollars by choosing an untracked service for an item where losing the package would hurt.

Can I use my own cheaper label if Depop gave me a label?

If the listing was sold using Depop Shipping, don't simply replace the Depop label with a cheaper outside label. Depop's current US shipping guidance says sellers using Depop Shipping must use the generated label. If you use a different shipping method instead, you may lose eligibility for Depop Protection and the order may be cancelled and refunded.

Depop Shipping → use the Depop-generated label·Other shipping → arrange your own tracked label

If the generated label appears wrong, missing or unusable, contact Depop rather than quietly switching shipping methods. Saving a few dollars on postage isn't worth creating a much bigger protection problem.

The weight you enter matters more than you think

One of the easiest shipping mistakes is weighing the item instead of the finished package. A hoodie might weigh 14 ounces by itself. Then you add the mailer, tissue, protective material, cardboard, and tape, and the final parcel can move into a different shipping tier.

That's why the better routine is: Item → package it → weigh the finished parcel → choose the shipping tier. Not: Item → guess the weight → choose the label → package it.

Depop's Terms state that sellers can be responsible for additional carrier charges associated with packages that exceed the weight allowed by the selected label. So if you're close to a weight boundary, don't guess. Put the finished package on the scale.

How long do I have to ship a Depop order?

There are two deadlines worth keeping separate.

Depop Protection deadline

For eligible US, UK and Australian sales using Depop Shipping, Depop's seller-protection requirements include shipping with the Depop label within 5 days of the transaction date.

US cancellation deadline

For US sellers, Depop's current guidance says that if an order hasn't been shipped within 11 days of purchase, the order will automatically be cancelled and the buyer refunded.

Don't interpret the 11-day period as: "I have eleven days before I need to worry." If you're relying on seller protection, the five-day requirement matters separately.

What if the buyer gives me a different address?

Don't simply send the parcel to the new address they typed into Depop messages. The address on the transaction matters for seller protection. If the buyer entered the wrong address, follow Depop's refund/re-purchase process rather than manually redirecting the parcel yourself.

Simple rule: Ship to the address on the Depop order. Not the address the buyer sends you afterward.

What does "free shipping" actually mean?

Free shipping means the buyer isn't paying the shipping charge you would otherwise show at checkout. It does not automatically mean shipping has no cost. The important question is: Who is absorbing the shipping cost under this particular listing or promotion?

If you are responsible for the label, that cost needs to be part of your selling calculation. And the impact can be very different depending on the item's price.

Same shipping cost. Completely different economics. That's why free shipping shouldn't automatically be treated as either good or bad.

Depop Shipping vs. Other Shipping: which should I use?

There isn't one universal answer. It depends on what matters most to you.

If you care most about...Shipping method to consider
A prepaid label generated through DepopDepop Shipping

Integrated trackingDepop Shipping

Shopping for your own carrier rateOther

You're not comparing label price alone. You're comparing: cost + convenience + tracking + protection requirements + your own workflow.

US, UK & Australia: Regional Shipping Rules

US sellers

For US Depop Shipping, Depop provides a prepaid USPS label. You can access the label through the sold order, with a QR code and printable label available. If the order uses Depop Shipping, use the Depop-generated label.

UK sellers

UK Depop Shipping uses Evri. Depop provides the shipping label and QR code, and the shipping is tracked.

Australia sellers: an important 2026 change

Depop's current Australia shipping guidance says Depop Shipping via Australia Post is the shipping option for new listings, while existing listings can remain on Other/manual shipping. Depop began this service through an Australia Post beta rollout in September 2026, so availability may depend on whether your account has access to the service.

The shipping cost sellers often forget

Shipping isn't only about the price printed on the label. It can also affect the economics of the transaction. For US Depop Payments, the payment-processing fee is calculated on the item price plus shipping and applicable taxes.

So if you charge the buyer $6 for shipping, that shipping amount is part of the processing calculation. For example: $30 item + $6 shipping = $36 processed amount before applicable taxes.

That's why shipping belongs inside your profit calculation rather than being treated as a completely separate number.

Your 5-minute post-sale routine

Save this routine and use it every time.

SOLD→Check shipping method→Check address→Pack item→Weigh finished package→Get correct label→Ship promptly→Keep proof

The five shipping mistakes most likely to hurt a seller

  1. Shipping to a different address because the buyer asked. Keep the shipping address aligned with the transaction.
  2. Replacing a Depop Shipping label with a cheaper outside label. If the order uses Depop Shipping, use the generated label.
  3. Guessing the final package weight. Weigh the finished parcel.
  4. Throwing away proof of shipment immediately. Keep your receipt and tracking information until the transaction is safely settled.
  5. Building your pricing around a temporary shipping promotion. Promotions change. Make sure the item still works economically when the promotion ends.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use the Depop shipping label?

If your listing was sold using Depop Shipping, you must use the Depop-generated label to maintain Depop Protection eligibility. If your listing uses "Other" shipping, you are responsible for buying your own tracked label.

Who pays for Depop shipping?

Usually, the buyer pays the shipping charge shown at checkout. However, if you offer "free shipping" or use a promotional free-shipping offer, the seller absorbs the actual cost of the shipping label.

Does shipping affect my Depop fees?

Yes. For US, UK, and Australian sellers using Depop Payments, the payment processing fee is calculated on the total transaction amount, which includes the item price, buyer-paid shipping, and applicable taxes.

What if the buyer asks me to ship to a different address?

Do not manually redirect the parcel to a new address sent in messages. To maintain seller protection, you must ship to the address shown on the official Depop order receipt. If the address is wrong, follow Depop's refund and re-purchase process.

How long do I have to ship a Depop order?

To maintain Depop Protection eligibility, you must ship with the Depop label within 5 days of the transaction date. In the US, if an order hasn't been shipped within 11 days of purchase, Depop will automatically cancel the order and refund the buyer.

What happens if my package weighs more than I entered?

Depop's Terms state that sellers can be responsible for additional carrier charges if a package exceeds the weight allowed by the selected label. Always weigh the finished, packaged parcel rather than guessing the weight of the item alone.

Final takeaway: shipping should not be the surprise after the sale

The easiest way to lose money or protection on Depop shipping is to treat shipping as something you'll figure out after the item sells. Instead, know your shipping setup before you list. Know approximately what the finished package will weigh. Know whether the buyer or seller is responsible for the shipping cost under the listing or promotion.

Then, when the sale happens, follow the shipping method attached to that order. For most sellers, the safest routine is wonderfully boring: check → pack → weigh → label → track → keep proof.

Shipping doesn't need to be complicated. It just needs to be deliberate. And if you want to know what the sale actually leaves in your pocket, don't stop at the label price. Put the item price, shipping and applicable fees together and look at the whole transaction. That's where the real number lives.
