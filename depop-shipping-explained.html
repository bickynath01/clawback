Depop Shipping Explained: Labels, Costs, Free Shipping & Who Really Pays

Last checked: August 15, 2026 · Reviewed by CLAWBACK Team. This guide is focused on Depop shipping mechanics, label rules, shipping costs, tracking and seller decisions. We separate official Depop policy from carrier-rate examples so you can tell what Depop requires from what a carrier may charge.

Shipping on Depop looks simple until it isn't. The buyer pays, right? Except Depop's processing fee bites that shipping too. First, the mechanics: how Depop shipping actually works

The short version: on a normal Depop sale, the buyer can be charged shipping at checkout, but who actually buys the label depends on the shipping method you selected when you listed the item. In the US, if you choose Depop Shipping via USPS, Depop generates the prepaid label and you must use that label. If you choose Other, you organize and pay for shipping yourself and add tracked shipping information to the order. Depop also allows US sellers to offer shipping discounts or free shipping on eligible listings. citeturn0search0turn0search9

That distinction matters because a common seller mistake is thinking, "I'll select Depop Shipping now and then buy a cheaper label somewhere else." Don't do that. Depop says that if you selected Depop Shipping, you must use the generated label; using a different shipping method can remove seller protection and may result in the order being canceled and refunded. citeturn0search0turn0search13

If you choose Other, the opposite applies: Depop does not automatically provide a prepaid label, so you organize and pay for shipping yourself. You must use tracked shipping, add the tracking to the receipt and mark the order as shipped. If tracking is not added within 10 days, Depop says the order will be canceled and the buyer refunded. citeturn0search0

And one more number matters for US sellers: Depop's current payment-processing fee is charged on the item price plus shipping costs and applicable taxes. So charging the buyer $6 for shipping does not make that $6 invisible to processing. citeturn0search8

The goal of this guide is simple: by the time you finish, you should know who pays, which label you are allowed to use, what happens if you ship your own way, when tracking matters, how free shipping affects your margin, and what to do when a shipping problem happens.

Letter #1 — "Does Depop take a cut of the shipping I charge?"

"I charge $6 shipping and the item is $30. Does the processing fee hit just the $30, or the $36 too? Because that changes what I actually make." — @VINTAGE.MARA, OHIO

Answer: the $36. US sellers pay no selling fee in 2026, but the 3.3% + $0.45 processing fee is charged on the item price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes. So on a $30 item + $6 shipping, processing is 3.3% of $36 + $0.45 = $1.64, not $1.44.

That extra twenty cents feels trivial until it's multiplied across a whole month. At 40 sales averaging $8 shipping, that's roughly $10–11 a month of processing-on-shipping that rarely gets budgeted for. Small bite — but it's real, and it's exactly why running the whole sale through the calculator before listing matters. (The full 2026 fee menu is broken down in how much Depop takes from a sale.)

Letter #2 — "Who pays for shipping on Depop, me or the buyer?"

"Every platform is different. On Depop, when I sell something, who is actually supposed to pay the postage?" — @FIRSTFLIP.JAY, TEXAS

Answer: usually the buyer — unless free shipping gets chosen. By default, the buyer pays whatever shipping cost is shown at checkout. Toggling "free shipping" flips that — the label comes out of the seller's margin instead. There's no Depop rule forcing either choice; it's a per-listing pricing decision.

For outbound shipping, the important question is simply which shipping method you selected and whether you offered free shipping. Return-shipping rules are separate from this guide.

Letter #3 — "Is free shipping worth it on Depop?"

"Everyone says free shipping makes items sell faster. But I sell $12-$18 tees. If I eat a $5 label, I'm basically working for free. What's the real play?" — @TEESTACK.LEO, FLORIDA

Answer: free shipping is a marketing cost, not a kindness — only worth it when the margin survives. Free shipping can lift visibility and conversion, since buyers filter for it and dislike surprise checkout costs. But the label doesn't disappear; it just moves from the buyer's line item into the seller's expenses.

Run Leo's numbers through the real rate card. A $15 tee at 4 oz carries a $4.91 USPS Ground Advantage label. Depop's only cut is the processing fee: 3.3% + $0.45 = about $0.95. If the buyer pays shipping, the seller keeps roughly $15 − $0.95 = $14.05, and the buyer covers the label separately. Offering free shipping instead means $14.05 − $4.91 = $9.14 kept — paying $4.91 just to look more attractive in search. On a $15 tee sourced for $4, that collapses the profit. On a $90 jacket with a $7.97 discounted prepaid label, the same move costs far less proportionally and may genuinely win the sale.

The fix: use free shipping selectively — on higher-priced items where the label is a small percentage — and on cheap items, either charge shipping outright or bake a couple dollars into the price and call it "free." Never offer it blindly; run the number first, the way the calculator above does.

Letter #5 — "Can I use my own label instead of Depop's?"

"The in-app label is convenient but I found cheaper rates on a third-party site. Am I allowed to buy my own label and still sell on Depop?" — @LABELHACK.RUI, NEW JERSEY

Answer: sometimes — it depends on what you selected on the listing. This is the shipping rule you do not want to get wrong.

If the listing is set to Depop Shipping in the US, Depop says you must use the prepaid USPS label generated by Depop. Buying a cheaper label somewhere else after the sale is not an equivalent substitute: Depop says you will not be covered by Depop Protection for sellers, and the order may be automatically canceled and refunded. citeturn0search0turn0search13

If the listing is set to Other, then yes: you organize and pay for your own shipping. Depop requires tracked shipping and says you must add the tracking information to the receipt and mark the order as shipped. Once you add tracking, Depop says it cannot be amended, so double-check the number before saving it. citeturn0search0

Seller-safe rule: don't choose Depop Shipping for the convenience of a prepaid label and then swap to an outside label to save money. Decide the shipping method at listing time and follow that method after the sale.

Letter #6 — "How do I pack clothes so they don't come back 'damaged'?"

"I had a buyer claim a hoodie arrived wet and stained and I lost the dispute. How do people pack so this never happens to them?" — @SECONDHAND.SAM, OREGON

Answer: pack for the worst day the postal system can have, and document everything. Most "damaged in transit" losses are preventable with a boring, repeatable routine — here's the checklist worth keeping taped near the packing station:

THE PACK-IT-LIKE-A-PRO CHECKLIST

  • Fold neatly and add tissue for higher-priced pieces (perceived value = fewer remorse returns).
  • Wrap in a waterproof poly mailer sized to the item — no giant bag flopping around.
  • For fragile or structured items, add a cardboard stiffener or bubble wrap.
  • Seal securely; reinforce edges with tape on boxes.
  • Include a small thank-you note — it genuinely reduces bad ratings and remorse returns.
  • Photograph the item folded AND inside the mailer before sealing (dispute evidence).
  • Weigh the final package and buy the correct label weight.

That photo step gives you useful evidence, but it is not a guarantee that you will win a Depop dispute. Depop Protection has eligibility requirements, and for US/UK sellers using Depop Shipping, the protection rules include using the Depop label and shipping within the required timeframe. Keep the photos, tracking and proof of shipment together so you can provide a clear record if something goes wrong. citeturn0search13

Letter #7 — "Does Depop ship internationally — and is it worth it?"

"I keep getting messages from buyers in other countries. Should I ship internationally or is that a scam magnet?" — @GLOBALTHRIFT.KIM, WASHINGTON

Answer: yes, but the exact shipping setup depends on where you are selling from. Depop's worldwide shipping guidance says sellers outside the US and UK can use their preferred shipping method, while sellers in the US and UK can enable worldwide shipping on their listings. International sellers are responsible for packing, shipping, required customs information and keeping proof of tracked postage. citeturn0search6turn0search4

For an international order, don't treat the domestic label table above as your international quote. Destination, parcel size, weight, carrier and customs requirements can change the real cost dramatically. Depop specifically tells worldwide sellers to factor in size, weight and distance when setting a shipping price and to use tracked shipping. citeturn0search6

There is also a current 2026 customs issue worth knowing about for shipments into the US: Depop says tariffs, duties or taxes may apply, and the process can change. Depending on the carrier and delivery terms, the buyer may be responsible for charges at delivery, or the seller may choose a delivered-duty-paid arrangement and price those costs into the sale. Check the current carrier rules before promising an international buyer a delivered price. citeturn0search11

Seller-safe rule: use tracked international shipping, keep proof of postage, declare information accurately, and never assume that a domestic USPS rate is a valid international quote.

Letter #8 — "What if my item is over 5 lbs or bulky?"

"I sell vintage coats and boots. Some packages hit 6-8 lbs and the label price shocks me. How do heavy sellers survive?" — @HEAVYROT.DEE, MICHIGAN

Answer: for heavy items, skip prepaid convenience and shop around for actual carrier rates. The rate card makes the trap visible: a 7 lb coat runs $17.11 on USPS Ground Advantage, while FedEx Economy's prepaid tier holds at $11.99 for 6–10 lb — a $5+ gap on every single sale. Comparing carrier and discount-service rates, considering slower ground tiers, and charging accurate shipping instead of offering free tends to be the difference between profit and a wasted sale. A $120 coat can absorb a $12 label; the buyer just needs to see it clearly at checkout, or the price needs to reflect it upfront.

What If I Don't Ship My Depop Order in Time?

Don't wait. This is one of the easiest shipping problems to prevent because Depop gives sellers a clear deadline.

For US sellers, Depop's current shipping guide says that if an order has not been shipped within 10 days of purchase, it will automatically be canceled and the buyer will receive a full refund. If you use Other shipping, Depop also says the tracking information must be added to the receipt within 10 days or the order will be canceled and the buyer refunded. citeturn0search0

There is an important second deadline for sellers who want Depop Protection: Depop's seller-protection policy says eligible US and UK sellers using Depop Shipping must send the item using a Depop Shipping label within 5 days of the transaction date. citeturn0search13

Seller relief: you don't need to memorize one vague "ship quickly" rule. Think of it as two separate clocks: 5 days for the Depop Protection shipping requirement and 10 days before a US order can be automatically canceled/refunded for not being shipped. Ship earlier than either deadline whenever possible.

What If the Depop Shipping Label Is Too Small for My Package?

Do not guess. Depop's Terms say sellers are responsible for reimbursing additional charges imposed by the shipping carrier, including charges related to an item weighing more than the maximum weight on the shipping label. citeturn0search4

Before you list a heavy item, weigh the finished package, not just the garment. Include the mailer or box and protective material. If the final package falls into a different shipping tier, update your listing before the sale rather than hoping the carrier will ignore the difference.

Don't do this: weigh a jacket at 14 oz on its own, choose a small shipping tier, then ship it in a box that pushes the finished parcel over the tier. The label is based on the shipment you actually hand to the carrier.

What If My Buyer Gives Me the Wrong Address?

Do not simply ship to a different address sent to you in messages. Depop's worldwide shipping guidance says that if the buyer entered the wrong address, the seller should refund the buyer and have the buyer update the address inside Depop before purchasing again. Sending to an address different from the one on the Depop receipt can void Depop Protection. citeturn0search6

This is one of those moments where a seller can feel pressured to "just send it here instead." Don't. The safest path is to keep the transaction address and shipping record aligned.

What If My Tracking Says Delivered but the Buyer Says It Never Arrived?

Keep your tracking and proof of shipment. Depop's Terms require valid proof of tracked shipping when an item does not arrive, and Depop's seller guidance specifically recommends tracked shipping when you arrange shipping yourself. citeturn0search4turn0search3

If you used Depop Shipping in the US, tracking is integrated because the label is generated through Depop. If you used Other, make sure the tracking number was added correctly to the receipt. citeturn0search0

A screenshot of the tracking page, your postage receipt and the Depop order details are worth keeping until the transaction is fully settled.

Does Free Shipping Mean Depop Pays My Shipping Cost?

No — don't confuse a buyer-facing "free shipping" offer with a free label for the seller.

When you offer free shipping, the buyer does not pay the shipping charge you would otherwise collect at checkout. The seller therefore needs to account for the label cost in the item's economics. Depop currently lets US sellers offer certain shipping discounts, including $2, $3, $4 off or free shipping on eligible listings through the iOS listing flow. citeturn0search9

There are also promotional free-shipping offers that are separate from your normal listing settings. For example, Depop's current US new-user promotion applies only to eligible users and qualifying Depop Shipping orders during the offer period; it should not be treated as a permanent "Depop always pays shipping" rule. citeturn0search1

Seller-safe calculation: before enabling free shipping, subtract the realistic label cost from your expected sale proceeds. Then account for any applicable transaction fees. If the remaining margin still makes sense, free shipping can be a pricing strategy. If it wipes out the profit, charge shipping instead.

What Should I Do Immediately After a Depop Sale?

THE 5-MINUTE SOLD-ORDER CHECKLIST

  • Open the sold receipt and confirm the buyer address.
  • Check the shipping method: Depop Shipping or Other.
  • Weigh the finished package, including packaging.
  • Use the correct label for the method selected on the listing.
  • Keep tracking/proof of shipment until the order is safely delivered and settled.

For US Depop Shipping, the generated USPS label is tracked automatically. For Other shipping, add the tracking number to the receipt and mark the order as shipped. citeturn0search0

The Long-Tail Questions Sellers Actually Ask

These are the questions to answer before a seller has to learn the expensive way:

  • Who pays shipping on Depop? Usually the buyer pays the shipping charge shown at checkout; the seller absorbs it when offering free shipping.
  • Does Depop charge a fee on shipping? For US Depop Payments, the 3.3% + $0.45 processing fee is calculated on item price plus shipping and applicable taxes. citeturn0search8
  • Can I use my own shipping label on Depop? Yes, when the listing is set to Other. If you selected Depop Shipping, use the Depop-generated label. citeturn0search0
  • Can I use USPS instead of Depop Shipping? If the listing is using Depop Shipping, the USPS label is generated by Depop and must be used. If the listing is set to Other, you arrange your own tracked shipping.
  • What happens if I don't ship my Depop order? In the US, Depop says an order not shipped within 10 days can be automatically canceled and refunded. citeturn0search0
  • How long do I have to ship a Depop order? Ship as soon as possible. Depop says most sellers ship within 2 days, and its seller-protection rules require eligible US/UK Depop Shipping orders to be sent within 5 days. citeturn0search0turn0search13
  • What if I put the wrong weight on my Depop shipping? Recheck the finished package before shipping. Depop's Terms say sellers can be responsible for additional carrier charges associated with overweight packages. citeturn0search4
  • Can I change the shipping method after the buyer purchases? Don't assume you can safely switch methods. If Depop Shipping was selected, use the generated label; if you need a different arrangement, contact Depop Support rather than silently changing the shipping method.
  • What if my buyer entered the wrong address? Refund and have the buyer update the address in Depop before purchasing again; shipping to a different address can void protection. citeturn0search6

The Shipping Desk decision flowchart

Standing there with the item in hand, this is the whole column compressed into ten seconds:

IF listing uses Depop Shipping → use the Depop-generated label; do not swap in an outside label.
IF listing uses Other → buy your own tracked label, add tracking to the receipt, and mark shipped.
IF you offer free shipping → treat the label as your cost and check the margin before listing.
IF the package is heavier than the selected tier → re-check the finished package before shipping; carrier charges can apply.
IF buyer gives you a different address → do not redirect the parcel; follow Depop’s refund/re-purchase process.
ALWAYS → ship promptly, keep tracking/proof, and keep the shipping method consistent with the listing.

The mistakes that cost the most (so they don't repeat)

Closing the desk with a few honest observations, since that's really the point of this column. Free shipping on a $14 tee "to be competitive" leaves roughly $7.40 after the $5.66 label and the $0.91 processing fee — well under minimum wage for the packing time involved. Guessing "about a pound" when the package is actually 1 lb 9 oz jumps the label from $7.48 to $12.83 and quietly eats the entire profit. And shipping on a cheap outside label without uploading the tracking means when a buyer claims it never arrived, there's nothing to point to. Every one of those is avoidable with the checklist and the rate card above. Shipping isn't glamorous, but it's exactly where small sellers quietly win or lose their month.

Conclusion: price the shipping or eat the shipping — the choice is real

Depop shipping looks simple on the surface and gets sneaky underneath: the buyer usually pays, Depop's processing fee bites whatever shipping gets charged, free shipping is a marketing cost dressed up as generosity, and the label is a flat tax that punishes cheap items hardest — with a carrier gap that rewards sellers who shop around. Weigh it, price it, photograph it, track it. Do those four things and shipping stops being a leak and becomes just another line that's fully under control.

FAQ

US sellers pay no selling fee in 2026, but the 3.3% + $0.45 processing fee is charged on item + shipping + tax — so shipping you charge gets processed too.
Who pays for shipping on Depop?
Usually the buyer; the seller pays only if free shipping is offered.
Does Depop provide shipping labels?
Yes, in-app prepaid labels, or an independent label with the tracking uploaded.
How much is a shipping label in 2026?
USPS Ground Advantage runs $4.91 (4 oz) to $20.32 (10 lb); discounted prepaid rates run ~$7.97 up to 5 lb and ~$11.99 for 6–10 lb.
Is free shipping worth it on Depop?
