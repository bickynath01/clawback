Depop Shipping Guide (2026): Labels, Costs & Rules

Quick answer: If the sold order uses Depop Shipping, use the label Depop generated (USPS in the US, Evri in the UK, Australia Post in Australia) — swapping in your own label can void seller protection. If the listing uses "Other" shipping, you arrange and pay for tracked shipping yourself. Either way, buyer-paid shipping is included in the payment-processing fee base, so your real shipping cost is the label price minus what the buyer paid.

You've made a sale on Depop. Great. But now you have questions: Who pays for shipping? Where is your shipping label? Should you use Depop Shipping or arrange your own? What happens if your parcel is heavier than expected? And if the package gets lost, could you lose both the item and the money?

Choosing a shipping method affects more than getting a parcel from your home to the buyer. It can affect your shipping costs, tracking responsibilities, seller protection eligibility, and how much money you keep from the sale.

The confusion often starts when sellers assume every Depop order follows the same rules. It doesn't. Available methods and requirements can differ by country, listing type, and rollout status. This guide explains how Depop shipping works, who pays, how to prepare and send an order, what to do when something goes wrong, and where to find the rules that apply to your location.

Quick answer: If your order uses Depop Shipping, follow the instructions and use the label generated for that order. If the listing uses "Other" shipping, you generally arrange shipping yourself, pay the carrier, and add tracking details as required. Before dispatching, check the sold order, delivery address, parcel dimensions, and the protection requirements for your region.

How Does Depop Shipping Work?

Depop shipping is the process of sending a sold item to its buyer. Depending on the shipping method available for your listing, Depop may provide a prepaid carrier label, or you may need to purchase shipping independently.

The first thing to check is the shipping method attached to your sold order. Do not assume you can switch methods after the sale without consequences.

The options available depend on your country and listing. Australia, for example, has introduced Depop Shipping in phases. The UK "Ship with Evri" terms also apply to a defined service area rather than every UK destination.

Your first four checks after a sale

  1. Shipping method: Does the sold order use Depop Shipping or "Other"?
  2. Delivery address: Is it the address shown on the order?
  3. Finished parcel: Have you checked its actual weight and dimensions after packaging?
  4. Dispatch and protection requirements: Are you following the deadlines and eligibility conditions?

Who Pays for Shipping on Depop?

The answer depends on the shipping arrangement. In a typical buyer-paid setup, the buyer pays the shipping amount shown at checkout. If you offer free shipping or a seller-funded discount, you cover some or all of the shipping cost instead.

Is Depop shipping free for sellers?

No—not automatically. Three situations are often confused:

  • Buyer-paid shipping: The buyer pays the shipping amount shown at checkout.
  • Seller-funded shipping: The seller covers some or all of the shipping cost.
  • Promotional free shipping: A qualifying order receives an offer under specific terms.

Depop's supplied US shipping-discount guidance says eligible US sellers using iOS can select discounts of $2, $3, or $4, or choose free shipping, for eligible Depop Shipping listings. Before pricing an item around a promotion, check whether the offer is still active and whether your listing and buyer qualify.

What if the buyer messages you a different address?

Do not casually redirect a sold order to an address supplied in messages. The Australian Shipping Policy specifically requires dispatch to the buyer's address using the prepaid Australia Post label generated through Depop. If the buyer needs an address change, check Depop's current order and support instructions before sending anything.

Depop Shipping in the United States

US sellers should follow the shipping instructions shown on the sold order and use the label generated through Depop when the listing uses Depop Shipping. The supplied US help page explains that Depop Shipping through USPS is tracked.

US seller protection and dispatch timing

The supplied Depop Protection terms describe a five-day dispatch condition for eligible sales, while the US shipping help material describes a ten-day cancellation condition. These are different rules serving different purposes. Do not assume that having ten days before cancellation means you can wait ten days and still meet the protection requirements.

Depop Shipping in the United Kingdom

UK sellers need to distinguish the general shipping process from the specific terms for Depop's "Ship with Evri" service. The UK Shipping Policy applies to UK-based sellers using "Ship with Evri" through Depop for shipments within the policy's defined UK Mainland.

Measure and weigh the finished package, not just the item. The supplied Evri terms list prohibited items such as liquids, perfumes, paints, certain batteries, and sharp instruments.

Depop Shipping in Australia

Australia has a separate rollout and shipping policy powered by Australia Post. Depop Shipping is being rolled out in phases and may not be available to every user at the same time.

Australian seller protection

The supplied beta terms state that trial sellers may qualify for Depop Protection on sales up to A$450 AUD when they use a Depop Shipping label and meet the eligibility requirements, including dispatch within five days.

How to Ship an Item on Depop: Step by Step

  1. Check the sold order: Confirm the shipping method, buyer's address, and any instructions.
  2. Package the item properly: Use a clean, sturdy package. Remove or cover old labels.
  3. Weigh and measure the finished parcel: Do not rely on the item's weight before packaging.
  4. Get the correct label: Retrieve the Depop label/QR code, or arrange your own tracked shipping.
  5. Drop off the parcel correctly: Follow carrier instructions.
  6. Keep your proof of dispatch: Retain the shipping receipt and tracking number.
  7. Monitor tracking and communicate: Keep communication inside Depop messaging.

Frequently Asked Questions About Depop Shipping

Who pays for shipping on Depop, the buyer or seller?

It depends on the shipping arrangement. In a buyer-paid setup, the buyer pays the amount shown at checkout. If the seller offers free shipping or funds a discount, the seller covers some or all of the shipping cost. Sellers using "Other" shipping arrange and pay for their own carrier service.

Is Depop shipping free for sellers?

No, not automatically. The buyer may pay shipping at checkout, the seller may fund shipping, or a qualifying order may receive a promotional offer. Check the shipping method and any applicable offer terms.

Do I have to use the Depop shipping label?

If your order uses Depop Shipping, use the label generated through Depop and follow the relevant rules. Using a different method can affect seller-protection eligibility. If your listing uses "Other," you arrange your own shipping.

Does Depop Shipping include tracking?

Depop Shipping is tracked. For manually arranged shipping, use a tracked service and add the tracking information as required.

What happens if I use a different label?

Using a different shipping method instead of the generated label can remove seller-protection eligibility. Check the requirements for your order before substituting a label.

How long do I have to ship a Depop order?

The deadline depends on the applicable rules. US help material describes automatic cancellation if shipping/tracking isn't completed within ten days. Seller-protection terms separately describe a five-day dispatch condition for eligible sales.

Why is my Depop shipping label not showing?

Check the order's shipping method first. If it uses Depop Shipping, look in the Sold or Selling Hub section and check your email. If it uses "Other," you may need to arrange shipping yourself.

Can I change the tracking number after adding it?

Tracking information cannot be amended once added in the relevant manual-shipping workflow. Double-check the number against your receipt before saving it.

Who pays for return shipping on Depop?

Responsibility can depend on the reason for the return and Depop's applicable return rules. Check the current policy for your case rather than assuming the buyer or seller always pays.

Does Depop Protection cover lost or damaged parcels?

Some sellers and orders may qualify, but protection is conditional. The shipping method, dispatch timing, transaction requirements, and item eligibility matter. It is not a guarantee of reimbursement for every loss.

Can I use Depop Shipping in Australia?

Depop Shipping in Australia is powered by Australia Post and has been introduced in phases. The general policy takes effect for new users from 6 October 2026 and existing users from 26 October 2026.

What are the UK Evri parcel limits?

Under the "Ship with Evri" terms for the defined UK Mainland, parcels must be no longer than 120 cm, weigh no more than 15 kg, and meet the combined-dimension limit of less than 245 cm.

How do I calculate what shipping actually costs me?

Compare the shipping amount collected from the buyer with the actual label or carrier cost you pay. The mini shipping calculator on this page shows the difference between those two amounts.
