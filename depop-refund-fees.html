Does Depop Charge a Fee When You Refund a Buyer? (The 2026 Truth)

It was 11pm when the message came in: "hey, the hoodie's cute but it's not really my style, can I return it?" I'd sold it for $40. I did the right thing — I issued the refund. Then I opened my balance and did the thing every Depop seller does at least once: I tried to figure out whether Depop had quietly kept a slice of a sale I never actually made.

If you've ever typed "does Depop charge for refunds" into Google at midnight, you're not paranoid. The answer is genuinely confusing, because Depop gives one fee back and keeps another. In this guide I'll walk you through exactly what comes back, what stays gone, what that weird "fee refund" line item means, who pays the return shipping, and — with three real receipts — what a Depop refund actually costs you.

The short answer: Depop does not charge you a brand-new "refund fee." But a refund is not free. Depop typically refunds its 10% selling fee, while the payment processing fee stays gone — and return shipping often lands on you. So every refund has a small, real cost that adds up.

The two fees Depop takes — and which one comes back

To understand refunds you first have to understand that Depop takes two separate bites out of every sale, and they behave completely differently when a refund happens.

FeeWhat it isWhen you refund a buyer…
Depop fee (10%)Commission on the item price + shipping.Usually credited back to you.
Payment processing feeCard-processing charge (varies by region, commonly ~2.9% + a fixed amount).Usually NOT returned.

That asymmetry is the entire story. The big, visible 10% comes back. The smaller, invisible processing fee does not. Which is exactly why sellers feel like they're being "charged to refund" even though, technically, no new fee was added. You're not paying a new charge — you're just not getting an old one back. Same sting.

What is a "fee refund" on Depop? (The line item, explained)

After you complete a refund, you may see a line in your activity that says "fee refund." A lot of sellers read that as another fee and panic. It's the opposite. A fee refund is Depop crediting back its 10% commission because the sale was reversed. Money coming back to you.

What you will not see is a matching line for the processing fee. That's the half that never returns. So when people ask "what is fee refund Depop" or "fee refund depop meaning," the honest translation is: "Depop gave you the 10% back, but kept the processing fee."

Does Depop charge a refund fee? (What it actually feels like)

Technically, no — there's no line item called "refund fee." But in practice, yes, every refund costs you something. On a $40 sale you might lose ~$1.40 of processing fee plus whatever return shipping you cover. It feels like a fee because it behaves like one: it's automatic, it's non-negotiable, and it's gone.

Who pays return shipping on Depop?

Unlike Poshmark (which issues labels) or eBay (which forces labels on "not as described"), Depop is more manual. For a not-as-described case, Depop support will usually require you, the seller, to cover return shipping if you want the item back and a clean resolution. Refuse, and they may refund the buyer and let them keep the item. For a changed-mind return you agree to accept, the buyer pays their own label. (Full platform-by-platform breakdown here.)

On Depop, your photos and description are your only contract. There's no corporate safety net — a dispute is basically you vs. the buyer, with support as referee.

Not as described vs. changed mind: which bucket are you in?

This distinction decides who eats the shipping. If the buyer claims not as described, expect to cover return shipping and face a forced full refund. If it's changed mind, you can simply decline — Depop doesn't force remorse returns. Knowing your bucket before you reply is the difference between a $1.46 refund and a $10+ refund.

How do I avoid Depop refund fees?

You can't eliminate refunds, but you can shrink the leak: photograph every flaw and tag before shipping; state measurements in inches, not vibes; pack like the carrier is angry; and price in the assumption that 1-in-15 sales refunds. And track every refund in the calculator so the leak becomes a number you can act on — and deduct.

Are Depop refund losses tax deductible?

Generally, yes for the cash costs. The non-refunded processing fee and the return shipping you pay are ordinary business expenses — deductible on Schedule C. Most sellers never track them, which means they overpay taxes on money they lost. (Full write-off guide here.)
