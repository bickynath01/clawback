How Much Does Depop Take From a Sale? Depop Fees Explained (2026)

I sold a jacket last month for $50 and did that thing every Depop seller does — I opened my balance before the buyer even confirmed, just to see what was left. $41.10. Then I remembered processing. Then I remembered the label I'd bought. By the time the dust settled I'd made about $36 on a "$50" sale. That gap between the number on the listing and the number in your pocket is what this guide is about.

Depop's fees aren't a secret, but they're scattered, and half the advice online is outdated. So I went through the current rules, ran the math on real sales, and broke down every bite Depop takes — plus the myths that make sellers underprice their stuff. If you've ever typed "how much does depop take from a sale" into Google, this is the plain-English answer.

The short answer: Depop takes a 10% selling fee on the total (item + shipping) plus a payment processing fee (roughly 2.9–3.3% + a small fixed amount, varies by country). There's no monthly fee and no listing fee. On a $50 sale, expect around $6.80 in fees before shipping.

Myth vs. reality: 8 things sellers get wrong

MYTH #1

"Depop takes 10% — that's the only fee."

REALITY

The 10% is the loud one, but the payment processing fee is the quiet second bite. On a $50 sale that's another ~$1.80. Sellers who budget only for the 10% are always surprised at payout. Always add both.

MYTH #2

"The 10% is only on the item price."

REALITY

No — it's on the total transaction, including the shipping you charge. Charge $8 shipping and Depop takes 10% of that too. This is why "free shipping" isn't free; you're paying the fee on a bigger total either way.

MYTH #3

"Depop charges a monthly fee."

REALITY

It doesn't. No subscription, no listing fees for standard selling. You only pay when you sell. That's genuinely seller-friendly compared to platforms with store subscriptions.

MYTH #4

"Shipping is profit."

REALITY

Shipping you collect gets taxed (see Myth #2), and the label you buy is a real cost. If you charge $8 shipping but the label costs $7.50, your "shipping profit" is fifty cents — before the 10% on that $8. Price shipping honestly, not optimistically.

MYTH #5

"You get paid instantly."

REALITY

Your balance updates when the sale completes, but the money isn't spendable until it lands in your bank, which typically takes 1–2 business days. Plan your cash flow around that lag, especially if you reinvest in inventory.

MYTH #6

"Boosts are worth it for everyone."

REALITY

Boosts only make sense if your margin survives the extra cost. On a $15 tee with $6 of fees, a boost can eat your whole profit. On a $120 jacket, it's often worth it. Run the margin first.

MYTH #7

"Refunds are free."

REALITY

They are not. Depop refunds the 10% but usually keeps the processing fee, and return shipping often lands on you. I wrote the full breakdown — does Depop charge a fee when you refund a buyer?

MYTH #8

"Depop fees are the same everywhere."

REALITY

The selling fee percentage and processing fees vary by country. A UK seller and a US seller don't pay identical numbers. Check your region on the official help page before you price.

How much does Depop take on a $50 sale? (Quick math)

$5 selling fee + ~$1.80 processing = ~$6.80, before your label. So a "$50" sale is really a $43.20 sale before shipping. If your item cost you $20 and the label $7, your true profit is ~$16. Know that number before you list, not after.

Is Depop worth it for sellers?

Honestly? For clothing and streetwear, yes — the audience is there and there's no monthly fee eating you alive while items sit. For heavy or cheap items, the flat label cost + percentage can squeeze you. Depop rewards higher-priced, shippable fashion. If you sell $10 trinkets, the math is against you. How to keep more of every sale

  1. Price in both fees, not just the 10%. Add ~3.5% for processing.
  2. Weigh items and price shipping from real label costs, not guesses.
  3. Avoid boosts on low-margin items; use them on $80+ pieces.
  4. Track every fee so at tax time the processing + shipping add up to a deduction. (Write-off guide.)
  5. Photograph flaws to cut not-as-described refunds, which are the real margin killer. (Are Depop fees refunded?)

Conclusion: the gap is the business

The difference between the listing price and what lands in your bank is where reselling is won or lost. Depop's two bites are fair if you price for them and brutal if you don't. Learn the menu, run the math on every listing, and the "$50 sale" stops being a mystery.
