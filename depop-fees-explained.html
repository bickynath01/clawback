How Much Does Depop Take From a Sale? Depop Fees Explained (2026)

A jacket sells for $50 and, like every Depop seller does, the balance gets checked before the buyer's even confirmed receipt — bracing for the old 10% hit. But the deduction that lands is smaller than expected: $2.10. Wait — what? If you're still pricing your listings around a 10% Depop fee, you're working off a rulebook that no longer applies to US sellers. That gap between what the forums say and what Depop actually charges in 2026 is the entire subject of this guide.

Here's the headline, straight from Depop's own "Seller fees and charges" page: no Depop selling fee is charged to sellers based in the UK, US or AUS. The only mandatory cut for a US seller is the payment processing fee — 3.3% + $0.45 per transaction, charged on item + shipping + tax. Half the reseller internet hasn't caught up yet. Below is the current rulebook, run against real sales, plus the myths that still make sellers overprice their fear and underprice their stuff.

The short answer (US sellers, 2026): Depop charges no selling fee — just a 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing fee on the total (item + shipping + tax). No monthly fee, no listing fee. On a $50 sale, expect around $2.10 in fees before shipping. Boosted listings add 12% (US/UK, new listings from 23 March 2026).

That's the general math — but the real number depends on your sale price, what shipping you charge, and whether the item was boosted. Plug in the real numbers below to see the real payout, not a rounded estimate.

Myth vs. reality: 8 things sellers still get wrong

MYTH #1

"Depop takes 10% of every sale."

REALITY

Not anymore — not for US, UK or AUS sellers. Depop's own fee page states no selling fee is charged in those three countries. The only mandatory cut is the processing fee (3.3% + $0.45 in the US). The 10% still applies to sellers based elsewhere, which is why the myth refuses to die. Know which rule applies to your account.

MYTH #2

"The processing fee is only on the item price."

REALITY

No — it's charged on the item price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes. Charge $8 shipping and the 3.3% bites that $8 too. Small bite, but it's real, and it's why "free shipping" isn't actually free.

MYTH #3

"Depop charges a monthly fee."

REALITY

It doesn't. No subscription, no listing fees for standard selling — payment only happens when something sells. Genuinely seller-friendly compared to platforms that charge store subscriptions regardless of sales.

MYTH #4

"Shipping is profit."

REALITY

Shipping charged still gets processed (see Myth #2), and the label itself is a real cost. Charge $8 shipping but pay $7.48 for the label, and the "shipping profit" is fifty-two cents — before the 3.3% on that $8. In 2026 the label, not the fee, is the biggest squeeze on small sales.

MYTH #5

"You get paid instantly."

REALITY

The balance updates when a sale completes, but the money isn't spendable until it lands in the bank — typically 1–3 business days later (and 2–10 working days is the release window after delivery). Plan cash flow around that lag. (Full payout timeline here.)

MYTH #6

"Boosts are worth it for everyone."

REALITY

The US/UK boosting fee rose to 12% for new listings from 23 March 2026. On a $15 tee that's $1.80 on top of processing — boost a few slow sellers and you've eaten the margin. On a $120 jacket it's often genuinely worth it. Run the margin first, boost second.

MYTH #7

"Refunds eat my fees."

REALITY

On Depop Payments, all fees reverse automatically when you refund — processing and boosting included. The real refund cost is the return label and any value drop in the item, not the fees. The full breakdown lives in the Depop refund fee calculator.

MYTH #8

"Depop fees are the same everywhere."

REALITY

They vary by region more than ever: UK 2.9% + £0.30 · US 3.3% + $0.45 · AUS 2.6% + $0.30 AUD · everywhere else 10% selling fee + PayPal processing. A thread written by a seller on another continent is describing a different fee schedule than yours.

How much does Depop take on a $50 sale? (Quick math)

$1.65 (3.3%) + $0.45 = $2.10. That's the entire platform cut on a $50 sale before the label — down from the ~$6.75 the old 10%-era math would predict. If the item cost you $20 and the label runs $7.48, true profit lands around $20.42. Worth knowing that number before listing, not after the sale's already done.

Is Depop worth it for sellers in 2026?

Honestly — more than the old advice suggests. With the selling fee gone for US sellers, Depop's mandatory cut is now among the lowest of the big four; the math usually dies on the label, not the fee. That makes Depop genuinely strong for higher-priced, easily shippable fashion, and still tricky for $10 trinkets where a $5–7 label is half the margin. How to keep more of every sale

  1. Price in the processing fee — add roughly 3.3% + $0.45 on top of your costs, not a phantom 10%.
  2. Weigh items and price shipping from real label costs, not guesses — the label is now the biggest fee on most sales.
  3. Skip boosts on low-margin items; at 12%, save them for $80+ pieces where the math actually works.
  4. Track every cost so tax season turns processing and shipping into real deductions. (Write-off guide.)
  5. Photograph flaws upfront to cut not-as-described refunds — the fees reverse, but the label and value drop don't. (Are Depop fees refunded?)

Conclusion: the fee changed — the habit shouldn't

The 10% era is over for US sellers, and sellers still pricing around it are either overcharging (and losing sales) or flinching at margins that are actually fine now. The new rulebook is simple: one small proportional bite, one flat label tax, and optional boosting that demands a margin check. Learn the real menu, run the math on every listing, and the "$50 sale" stops being a mystery — it becomes a number that was known in advance.
