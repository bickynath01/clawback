PRIVACY POLICY

The short version: we collect nothing, because there is nothing to collect.

What we store

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your sale price, return details, and results are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. Close the tab and they're gone.

Cookies & ads

CLAWBACK itself sets no tracking cookies. If we ever enable display advertising (e.g., Google AdSense/Ezoic), those third-party networks may use cookies to serve ads; you can opt out via their settings. Affiliate links, if present, may include a tracking id for the referral but carry none of your personal data.

Third-party links

We link to official marketplace help pages and, occasionally, partner tools. Those sites have their own privacy policies; we don't control and aren't responsible for them.
